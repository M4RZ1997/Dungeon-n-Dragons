% ADDITIONAL FEATURES & TRAITS
\AdditionalFeaturesAndTraits{
	S.C.O.U.T-1 still bears many signs of its original life as an agricultural construct. Its weathered frame is patched with mismatched metal plates, old mounting points, and remnants of straw and cloth once used to give it the appearance of a field scarecrow. Over decades of field service, these simple components were gradually replaced or supplemented by improvised reconnaissance equipment. Its head now houses an extensive sensory array capable of tracking movement, heat, sound, and subtle environmental changes with remarkable precision. Retractable under-forearm crossbows have been fitted beneath both arms, allowing the hands to remain fully functional while providing immediate ranged support. Numerous pouches, climbing hooks, survey tools, and camouflage materials hang from its frame, while a dark, weathered cloak helps break up its silhouette. Almost every modification appears hand-fitted rather than factory-made, giving S.C.O.U.T-1 the unmistakable appearance of a machine that has been continually adapted rather than replaced.
}

% BACKGROUND
\Characterbackground{
	Originally designated FC-17, S.C.O.U.T-1 was among the first generation of constructs built in Auchtertool roughly two decades ago. Created as a simple agricultural machine, FC-17 spent its earliest years tending fields, hauling equipment, and standing watch over crops. As newer and more sophisticated farming constructs gradually replaced the ageing model, its durable frame was spared from dismantling and instead repurposed for reconnaissance.

	Over the following years, FC-17 was repeatedly modified with improved sensors, stealth equipment, surveying tools, and concealed weaponry until little remained of its original purpose beyond the weathered scarecrow-like frame. Reclassified as S.C.O.U.T-1, it became one of Auchtertool's most experienced surveillance units, serving in covert missions and long-range reconnaissance throughout the region. Among its most significant successes was helping locate the Dread-Witch Queen of Cellardyke's hidden refuge within Ravenscraig Castle, providing crucial intelligence for the campaign against her forces.

	Though technologically outdated by modern Auchtertool standards, S.C.O.U.T-1 remains in active service because few newer constructs can match the field experience it has accumulated over years of observation, infiltration, and survival.
}

\chapter*{Features, Magic Items and Spells}

\section*{Harvestborn Traits}
\subsection*{Culling}
When you damage a Bloodied creature, you can deal an extra 1d12 Necrotic damage to it. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
\subsection*{Gift of the Green}
As a Bonus Action, you can touch the ground and choose a creature also touching the ground within 30 feet of yourself. That creature can expend and roll one Hit Points Die, and it regains a number of Hit Points equal to the number rolled plus your Proficiency Bonus. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus. You regain one expended use when you finish a Short Rest, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
\subsection*{Jack-O-Lantern}
You know the Dancing Lights cantrip. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it (choose the ability when you select this species).
\subsection*{Scarecrow Nature}
You don't require air, food, or drink.
\subsection*{Watchful Rest}
You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. Moreover, you can finish a Long Rest in 4 hours if you spend those hours in an inactive, motionless state, during which you retain consciousness.\\

\section*{Feats}
\subsection*{Hunter of Hunters}
You are adept at hunting monstrous prey, granting you the following benefits.
\paragraph*{Dead Aim} As a Bonus Action, you can scrutinize a creature to spot its vulnerable points. The next time you deal damage to the creature before the start of your next turn, you ignore the creature's damage Resistances, if any.
\paragraph*{Studied Quarry} You have Advantage on ability checks made to recall information about a creature.

\section*{Ranger Traits}
\subsection*{Favored Enemy}
\textbf{3 Uses per Long Rest}\\
You always have the \textit{Hunter's Mark} spell prepared. You can cast it twice without expending a spell slot, and you regain all expended uses of this ability when you finish a Long Rest.
\subsection*{Weapon Mastery}
Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons of your choice with which you have proficiency, such as Longbows and Shortswords.

Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can change the kinds of weapons you chose. For example, you could switch to using the mastery properties of Scimitars and Longswords
\subsubsection*{Light Crossbow (Slow)}
If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn't exceed 10 feet.
\subsubsection*{Scythe (Cleave)}
If you hit a creature with a melee attack roll using this weapon, you can make a melee attack roll with the weapon against a second creature within 5 feet of the first that is also within your reach. On a hit, the second creature takes the weapon's damage, but don't add your ability modifier to that damage unless that modifier is negative. You can make this extra attack only once per turn.

\paragraph*{Expertise} Choose one of your skill proficiencies with which you lack Expertise. You gain Expertise in that skill.
\paragraph*{Languages} You know two languages of your choice from the language tables in chapter 2.
\subsection*{Fighting Style}
You gain a Fighting Style feat of your choice.
\subsubsection*{Archery}

You are also adept at evading creatures that rely on Darkvision. While entirely in Darkness, you have the Invisible condition to any creature that relies on Darkvision to see you in that darkness.

You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

\subsection*{Parallax Crossbows}
\textit{Weapon (Pair of Integrated Crossbows), Rare (requires Attunement by a Construct)}\\
\textbf{Damage:} 1d6 Piercing\\
\textbf{Range:} 80/320 ft.\\
\textbf{Properties:} Light, Loading\\
\textbf{Weapon Mastery:} Slow\\
The Parallax Crossbows are a matched pair of compact ranged weapons designed for reconnaissance constructs. Each crossbow is mounted beneath one forearm, leaving the wielder's hands completely free. Sophisticated targeting mechanisms link the two weapons together, allowing information gathered from one shot to guide the next and progressively narrow down a target's position.

While attuned to the Parallax Crossbows, you gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with them. The pair counts as a single magic item for the purpose of Attunement.\\
\paragraph*{Integrated Loading System} Each crossbow contains its own internal ammunition feed and automatically loads its next bolt after being fired. You do not require a free hand to load either weapon. Each crossbow retains the Loading property and can therefore be fired only once as part of the same Action, Bonus Action, or Reaction. A creature with Extra Attack can consequently fire each crossbow once during the same Attack action.\\
\paragraph*{Triangulation Protocol} When you hit a creature with either Parallax Crossbow, that creature becomes Surveyed until the start of your next turn. Only one creature can be Surveyed at a time; surveying another creature ends the effect on the previous target.

While a creature is Surveyed, all attacks you make against it with either Parallax Crossbow ignore Half Cover and Three-Quarters Cover and deal an additional 1d6 Piercing damage on a hit. You also always know the creature's location while it is within 120 feet of you, it cannot become Hidden from you, and being Invisible does not impose Disadvantage on your attack rolls against it.
