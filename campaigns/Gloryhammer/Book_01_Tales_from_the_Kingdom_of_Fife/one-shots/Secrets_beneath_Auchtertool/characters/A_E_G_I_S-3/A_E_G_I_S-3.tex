% ADDITIONAL FEATURES & TRAITS
\AdditionalFeaturesAndTraits{
	A.E.G.I.S-3 is built around a tall, agile warforged frame designed for rapid interception rather than brute-force defense. Reinforced armor plating protects its core systems without sacrificing mobility, while servo-assisted joints allow it to reposition quickly and react to threats with unusual precision. Integrated into its back is a compact Alchemical Crucible System, containing three sealed reservoirs for Spellshine Ointment, Tenmen Tincture, and Fleshknit Phosphate. Fine injector lines run beneath the armor and distribute each compound directly through the chassis when activated. These substances can temporarily harden its plating, enhance its physical response, or accelerate repairs to damaged components. Its entire construction reflects a military design philosophy centered on speed, controlled aggression, and protecting allies before a threat can reach them.
}

% BACKGROUND
\Characterbackground{
	A.E.G.I.S-3 was created as part of Auchtertool's Alchemically Enhanced Guardian \& Intervention Sentinel program, a military initiative intended to combine warforged durability with rapid-response battlefield tactics and experimental alchemical augmentation. Unlike heavier security constructs, A.E.G.I.S.-3 was designed to intercept threats quickly, protect vulnerable personnel, and remain effective during prolonged engagements through the use of its integrated Crucible System.

	Its abilities were tested under genuine combat conditions when A.E.G.I.S.-3 was deployed alongside the Questlords of Inverness during the struggle against the forces of the Dread-Witch Queen of Cellardyke. The engagement provided valuable field data and proved the effectiveness of both its combat protocols and alchemical systems.
}

\chapter*{Features, Magic Items and Spells}

\section*{Warforged Traits}
\subsection*{Construct Resilience}
You have Resistance to Poison damage. You also have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.
\subsection*{Integrated Protection}
You gain a +1 bonus to your Armor Class. In addition, armor you have donned can't be removed against your will while you're alive.
\subsection*{Sentry's Rest}
You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. You can finish a Long Rest in 6 hours if you spend those hours in an inactive, motionless state. During this time, you appear inert but remain conscious.
\subsection*{Specialized Design}
\textit{Acrobatics Proficiency, Drum Proficiency}\\
You gain one skill proficiency and one tool proficiency of your choice.
\subsection*{Tireless}
You don't gain Exhaustion levels from dehydration, malnutrition, or suffocation.

\section*{Feats}
\subsection*{Savage Attacker}
You've trained to deal particularly damaging strikes. Once per turn when you hit a target with a weapon, you can roll the weapon's damage dice twice and use either roll against the target.
\subsection*{Mobile Combatant}
You gain the following benefits.

\paragraph*{Slippery} When you take the Attack action, your Speed increases by 10 feet and Opportunity Attacks have Disadvantage against you until the end of your turn.

\section*{Fighter Traits}
\subsection*{Fighting Style}
\textbf{Mobile Combatant}\\
You have honed your martial prowess and gain a Fighting Style feat of your choice. Defense is recommended.

Whenever you gain a Fighter level, you can replace the feat you chose with a different Fighting Style feat.
\subsection*{Second Wind}
\textbf{Heal: 1d10 + \intcalcAdd{0}{\LevelValue}, 3 Uses}\\
You have a limited well of physical and mental stamina that you can draw on. As a Bonus Action, you can use it to regain Hit Points equal to 1d10 plus your Fighter level.

You can use this feature twice. You regain one expended use when you finish a Short Rest, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

When you reach certain Fighter levels, you gain more uses of this feature, as shown in the Second Wind column of the Fighter Features table.
\subsection*{Weapon Mastery}
\textbf{4 Weapon Masteries Known}\\
Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of three kinds of Simple or Martial weapons of your choice. Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can practice weapon drills and change one of those weapon choices.

When you reach certain Fighter levels, you gain the ability to use the mastery properties of more kinds of weapons, as shown in the Weapon Mastery column of the Fighter Features table.
\subsubsection*{Sap (Spear)}
If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
\subsubsection*{Slow (Javelin)}
If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn't exceed 10 feet.
\subsubsection*{Swift (Sabre)}
Swift weapons help you recover quickly from missed attacks. When you miss with a Swift weapon, you have Advantage on the first attack roll you make with the weapon against the same target before the end of your next turn. If that attack hits, it also deals an extra 1d4 damage of the weapon's normal damage type.
\subsubsection*{Vex (Shortbow)}
If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to the creature, you have Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn.
\subsection*{Action Surge}
You can push yourself beyond your normal limits for a moment. On your turn, you can take one additional action, except the Magic action.

Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest. Starting at level 17, you can use it twice before a rest but only once on a turn.
\subsection*{Tactical Mind}
You have a mind for tactics on and off the battlefield. When you fail an ability check, you can expend a use of your Second Wind to push yourself toward success. Rather than regaining Hit Points, you roll 1d10 and add the number rolled to the ability check, potentially turning it into a success. If the check still fails, this use of Second Wind isn't expended.\\
\subsection*{Living Crucible}
\subsubsection*{Compound Creator}
You learn to create alchemical compounds toxic to others but empowering to you.

\paragraph*{Compounds} You learn three compounds of your choice from the "Compound Options" section below. You learn two additional compounds of your choice when you reach Fighter levels 7, 10, and 15. Each time you learn new compounds, you can also replace one compound you know with a different one.
\subparagraph*{Fleshknit Phosphate} For 1 minute, you regain Hit Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus at the start of each of your turns if you have at least 1 Hit Point.
\subparagraph*{Spellshine Ointment} For 1 minute, you have Resistance to Force, Necrotic, Psychic, and Radiant damage.
\subparagraph*{Tenmen Tincture} For 1 hour, you count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity, and when you attack with a Melee weapon whose mastery property you can use, you can replace that property with the Topple property for that attack.
\paragraph*{Creating} Whenever you finish a Long Rest while holding Alchemist's Supplies, you can use that tool to magically produce any number of compounds. The compound appears in a vial, and the vial vanishes when the compound is consumed or poured out. If any compound remains when you finish a Long Rest, the compound and its vial vanish.
\paragraph*{Consuming} As a Bonus Action, you can consume one compound. You can consume a number of compounds up to one plus your Constitution modifier (minimum of one). Once you reach this limit, you cannot benefit from more compounds until you finish a Long Rest.

You can benefit from multiple compounds at the same time, but consuming multiple vials of the same compound provides no additional effects.

Only you can benefit from your compounds. Any other creature that consumes a compound must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 plus your Intelligence modifier plus Proficiency Bonus) or have the Poisoned condition for 1 minute.
\subsubsection*{Student of Alchemy}
Your long nights of vigilance have left your senses attuned to signs of danger, granting you the following benefits.

You gain Alchemist's Supplies, and you have proficiency with it. In addition, your Proficiency Bonus is doubled for ability checks with Alchemist's Supplies.\\
\subsection*{Extra Attack}
You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.
\subsection*{Tactical Shift}
Whenever you activate your Second Wind with a Bonus Action, you can move up to half your Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

\part*{Magic Items}
\subsection*{Intervention Sabre}
\textit{Weapon (Sabre), Rare (requires Attunement by a Warforged)}\\
\textbf{Damage:} 1d8 Slashing\\
\textbf{Properties:} Finesse\\
\textbf{Weapon Mastery:} Swift\\
You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.\\
\paragraph*{Threat Designation} When you hit a creature with the Intervention Sabre, you can designate it as a threat until the start of your next turn. While designated, the creature has Disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you.\\
\paragraph*{Intervention Protocol} When a creature designated by the sabre makes an attack against another creature, you can use your Reaction to move up to half your Speed toward it without provoking Opportunity Attacks. If you end this movement within reach of the designated creature, you can make one attack against it with the Intervention Sabre as part of the same Reaction.
