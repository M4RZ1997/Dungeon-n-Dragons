% ADDITIONAL FEATURES & TRAITS
\AdditionalFeaturesAndTraits{
	L.O.R.E-3 is built as a dedicated arcane research platform, its slender warforged frame incorporating numerous lenses, runic sensors, and calibration instruments for examining unfamiliar magical phenomena. Its hands are exceptionally precise, fitted with retractable styluses and fine engraving tools suited to calligraphy, cartography, and the transcription of delicate runes. L.O.R.E-3 rarely operates without the Codex Veritas, an awakened spellbook that serves as both companion and primary research archive, responding to the construct as new knowledge is recorded within its pages. Rotating optical lenses and supplementary articulated instruments allow L.O.R.E-3 to inspect objects from several angles at once, giving the unit the unmistakable appearance of a scholar built to catalogue anything that can be understood - and investigate anything that cannot.
}

% BACKGROUND
\Characterbackground{
	L.O.R.E-3 was constructed approximately seven years ago as part of Auchtertool's effort to create a dedicated platform for the study of arcane phenomena. Rather than being designed for labour or warfare, the unit was built around an unusually sophisticated cognitive core intended to process magical theory, historical records, runic systems, and complex patterns far beyond the capabilities of most constructs. The result was one of the most intelligent machines ever produced by the Auchtertool Foundries.

	Since its activation, L.O.R.E-3 has served the city's artificers as an arcane diagnostician, examining enchanted objects, translating obscure inscriptions, documenting magical anomalies, and assisting with research into technologies whose functions are not immediately understood. Its work is closely supported by the awakened spellbook Codex Veritas, which acts as both research archive and companion, preserving the discoveries accumulated throughout the unit's service.
}

% TREASURES
\Treasure{
	\textbf{Untranslated Rune Shard:} A fragment of inscribed black stone recovered during an earlier investigation. Despite years of study, L.O.R.E-3 has never deciphered it and refuses to classify the matter as closed.\\\\
	\textbf{First Finished Map:} A carefully preserved hand-drawn map produced shortly after L.O.R.E-3 learned cartography. Its measurements are slightly imperfect, but the construct keeps it as evidence of knowledge acquired rather than installed.\\\\
	\textbf{Vial of Archivist's Ink:} A small sealed bottle of deep blue ink mixed personally by the artificer who first taught L.O.R.E-3 calligraphy. It is rarely used and reserved only for discoveries considered truly significant.
}

\chapter*{Features, Magic Items and Spells}

\section*{Warforged Traits}
\subsection*{Construct Resilience}
You have Resistance to Poison damage. You also have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.
\subsection*{Integrated Protection}
You gain a +1 bonus to your Armor Class. In addition, armor you have donned can't be removed against your will while you're alive.
\subsection*{Sentry's Rest}
You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. You can finish a Long Rest in 6 hours if you spend those hours in an inactive, motionless state. During this time, you appear inert but remain conscious.
\subsection*{Specialized Design}
\textit{Acrobatics Proficiency, Drum Proficiency}\\
You gain one skill proficiency and one tool proficiency of your choice.
\subsection*{Tireless}
You don't gain Exhaustion levels from dehydration, malnutrition, or suffocation.

\section*{Feats}
\subsection*{Magic Initiate (Wizard)}
You gain the following benefits.
\subsubsection*{Two Cantrips}
\textbf{Magnetobolt, Mending}\\
You learn two cantrips of your choice from the Cleric, Druid, or Wizard spell list. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (choose when you select this feat).
\subsubsection*{Level 1 Spell}
\textbf{Binding Pledge}\\
Choose a level 1 spell from the same list you selected for this feat's cantrips. You always have that spell prepared. You can cast it once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast the spell using any spell slots you have.
\subsubsection*{Spell Change}
Whenever you gain a new level, you can replace one of the spells you chose for this feat with a different spell of the same level from the chosen spell list.

\section*{Wizard Traits}
\subsection*{Ritual Adept}
You can cast any spell as a Ritual if that spell has the Ritual tag and the spell is in your spellbook. You needn't have the spell prepared, but you must read from the book to cast a spell in this way.
\subsection*{Arcane Recovery}
You can regain some of your magical energy by studying your spellbook. When you finish a Short Rest, you can choose expended spell slots to recover. The spell slots can have a combined level equal to no more than half your Wizard level (round up), and none of the slots can be level 6 or higher. For example, if you're a level 4 Wizard, you can recover up to two levels' worth of spell slots, regaining either one level 2 spell slot or two level 1 spell slots.

Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
\subsection*{Scholar}
\textbf{Arcana Expertise}\\
While studying magic, you also specialized in another field of study. Choose one of the following skills in which you have proficiency: Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, or Religion. You have Expertise in the chosen skill.
\subsection*{Order of Scribes}
\subsubsection*{Wizardly Quill}
As a bonus action, you can magically create a Tiny quill in your free hand. The magic quill has the following properties:
\begin{itemize}
	\item The quill doesn't require ink. When you write with it, it produces ink in a color of your choice on the writing surface.
	\item The time you must spend to copy a spell into your spellbook equals 2 minutes per spell level if you use the quill for the transcription.
	\item You can erase anything you write with the quill if you wave the feather over the text as a bonus action, provided the text is within 5 feet of you.
\end{itemize}
This quill disappears if you create another one or if you die.
\subsubsection*{Awakened Spellbook}
Using specially prepared inks and ancient incantations passed down by your wizardly order, you have awakened an arcane sentience within your spellbook.

While you are holding the book, it grants you the following benefits:
\begin{itemize}
	\item You can use the book as a spellcasting focus for your wizard spells.
	\item When you cast a wizard spell with a spell slot, you can temporarily replace its damage type with a type that appears in another spell in your spellbook, which magically alters the spell's formula for this casting only. The latter spell must be of the same level as the spell slot you expend.
	\item When you cast a wizard spell as a ritual, you can use the spell's normal casting time, rather than adding 10 minutes to it. Once you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.
\end{itemize}
If necessary, you can replace the book over the course of a short rest by using your Wizardly Quill to write arcane sigils in a blank book or a magic spellbook to which you're attuned. At the end of the rest, your spellbook's consciousness is summoned into the new book, which the consciousness transforms into your spellbook, along with all its spells. If the previous book still existed somewhere, all the spells vanish from its pages.
\subsection*{Memorize Spell}
Whenever you finish a Short Rest, you can study your spellbook and replace one of the level 1+ Wizard spells you have prepared for your Spellcasting feature with another level 1+ spell from the book.

\subsection*{Codex Veritas}
\textit{Wondrous Item (Spellbook), Rare (requires Attunement by a Wizard)}\\
The Codex Veritas is an awakened spellbook bound in reinforced brass and dark leather, its pages threaded with faintly luminous arcane script. Rather than remaining fixed upon the page, its writing constantly rearranges itself as the book compares new information against everything previously recorded within it. When accompanying its wielder, the Codex frequently hovers nearby, opening itself to relevant passages or filling its margins with observations of its own.

The Codex functions as your spellbook and Spellcasting Focus and possesses all properties granted by the Awakened Spellbook feature. While using the Codex Veritas as your Spellcasting Focus, you gain a +1 bonus to your spell attack rolls and Spell Save DC.\\
\paragraph*{Living Index} Whenever you make an Intelligence (Arcana, History, Investigation, or Religion) check to examine a magical object, spell, rune, inscription, or other arcane phenomenon, the Codex automatically searches its accumulated records for relevant information. You have Advantage on the check.\\
\paragraph*{Veritas Analysis} When you cast Identify using the Codex, its analysis penetrates magical obfuscation normally concealed from the spell. In addition to the information normally revealed, you learn whether the target carries a curse, possesses concealed magical properties, or requires an unusual condition for its powers to function. If sufficient evidence exists, the Codex may also provide information concerning the object's approximate origin or purpose.\\
\paragraph*{Cross-Reference} When you encounter writing, symbols, or magical notation that you cannot immediately understand, you can spend 10 minutes allowing the Codex to compare them against its accumulated records. The DM tells you whether the markings resemble any language, magical tradition, culture, or system previously known to the Codex. This property does not automatically translate unknown writing or solve ciphers, but may provide clues to their origin or structure.
