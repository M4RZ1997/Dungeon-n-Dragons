% ADDITIONAL FEATURES & TRAITS
\AdditionalFeaturesAndTraits{
	F.I.X-2 is a compact repair construct built around a dense array of diagnostic instruments, articulated tools, and field-maintenance equipment. Multiple lenses and sensors allow it to inspect damaged machinery with exceptional precision, while retractable probes, magnetic grips, and specialized tool mounts let it perform delicate repairs without returning to a workshop. Its back-mounted pressure core powers an assortment of auxiliary systems and provides direct interfaces for servicing other constructs. F.I.X-2 is commonly accompanied by two smaller support machines: a sturdy Steel Defender used for protection and heavy repair assistance, and a tiny winged Homunculus Servant suited for scouting, carrying components, and reaching inaccessible mechanisms. Despite its cluttered appearance, nearly every tool and compartment on F.I.X-2 serves a specific purpose, reflecting a design built entirely around keeping other machines operational in the field.
}

% BACKGROUND
\Characterbackground{
	F.I.X-2 was developed as part of the Field Intervention Expert program, a compact support line created to accompany Auchtertool's heavier industrial and military constructs into environments where returning to a workshop was impractical. The series was designed alongside experimental machines such as the B.R.E.A.C.H. units, providing emergency repairs, diagnostics, replacement parts, and technical assistance directly in the field.

	As one of the more experimental F.I.X. models, Unit 2 was fitted with an unusually broad assortment of modular tools, arcane interfaces, and repair systems. Its capabilities were further expanded through the addition of a Steel Defender for protection and heavy maintenance work, as well as a small Homunculus Servant capable of reaching mechanisms inaccessible to larger constructs. Together, the three function as a self-contained mobile repair crew.

	F.I.X.-2 has since spent much of its service maintaining constructs during hazardous expeditions, industrial accidents, and field deployments throughout Auchtertool.
}

\chapter*{Features, Magic Items and Spells}

\section*{Autognome Traits}
\subsection*{Armored Casing}
\textbf{AC: \intcalcAdd{13}{\calculateModifier{\DexterityScoreValue}}}\\
You are encased in thin metal or some other durable material. While you aren't wearing armor, your base Armor Class is 13 + your Dexterity modifier.
\subsection*{Built for Success}
You can add a d4 to one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw you make, and you can do so after seeing the d20 roll but before the effects of the roll are resolved. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
\subsection*{Healing Machine}
If the Mending spell is cast on you, you can spend a Hit Die, roll it, and regain a number of hit points equal to the roll plus your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1 hit point). In addition, your creator designed you to benefit from several spells that preserve life but that normally don't affect Constructs: Cure Wounds, Healing Word, Mass Cure Wounds, Mass Healing Word, and Spare the Dying.
\subsection*{Mechanical Nature}
You have resistance to poison damage and immunity to disease, and you have advantage on saving throws against being paralyzed or poisoned. You don't need to eat, drink, or breathe.
\subsection*{Sentry's Rest}
When you take a long rest, you spend at least 6 hours in an inactive, motionless state, instead of sleeping. In this state, you appear inert, but you remain conscious.

\section*{Feats}
\subsection*{Altered}
You have been changed through magic, science, or a volatile blend of the two. You have a blatant physical augmentation of your choice from the options below. The augmentation is obvious - such as with stitches, grafts of other creature's body parts, or implants - unless disguised.
\subsubsection*{Night Vision}
You have Darkvision with a range of 60 feet. If you already have Darkvision, its range increases by 30 feet.

\subsubsection*{Creating an Item}
When you finish a Long Rest, you can create one or two different magic items if you have Tinker's Tools in hand. Each item is based on one of the plans you know for this feature.

If a created item requires Attunement, you can attune yourself to it the instant you create it. If you decide to attune to the item later, you must do so using the normal process for Attunement.

When you reach certain Artificer levels specified in the Magic Items column of the Artificer Features table, the number of magic items you can create at the end of a Long Rest increases. Each item you create must be based on a different plan you know.

You can't have more magic items from this feature than the number shown in the Magic Items column of the Artificer Features table for your level. If you try to exceed your maximum number of magic items for this feature, the oldest item vanishes, and then the new item appears.
\subsubsection*{Duration}
A magic item created by this feature functions as the normal magic item, except its magic isn't permanent; when you die, the magic item vanishes after 1d4 days. If you replace a plan you know with a new plan, any magic item created with the replaced plan immediately vanishes.

If an item that you created with this feature is a container, such as a Bag of Holding, and it vanishes, its contents harmlessly appear in and around its space.
\subsubsection*{Spellcasting Focus}
You can use any Wand or Weapon created by this feature as a Spellcasting Focus in lieu of using a set of Artisan's Tools.
