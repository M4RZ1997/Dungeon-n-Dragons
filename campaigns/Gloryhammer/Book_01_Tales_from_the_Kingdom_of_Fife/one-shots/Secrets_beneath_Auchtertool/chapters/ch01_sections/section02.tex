\section*{Expedition Briefing}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Expedition Briefing}
{\entryfont With the entrance secured and preliminary excavation complete, the Artificer Guild has assembled a small team of specialized constructs to conduct the first proper expedition below Auchtertool. No one has travelled farther than the exposed section immediately beneath the plaza, and little is known about the age, extent, or original purpose of the passage.

The expedition has deliberately been composed of units suited to excavation, reconnaissance, security, maintenance, infiltration, and arcane analysis. Their task is not merely to explore, but to determine whether the discovery presents a danger to Auchtertool and whether anything beneath the city might prove useful in the coming war.}
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	A soot-stained artificer stands near the edge of the excavation, clutching a clipboard beneath one arm while several measuring devices tick and whir around him.

	"Right. The good news is that we have discovered something beneath Auchtertool. The less good news is that we have absolutely no idea what it is."

	He gestures toward the darkness below.

	"You are the first expedition. Find out where that passage leads, determine whether anything down there intends to kill us, and document anything unusual along the way. Machinery, inscriptions, artefacts, structural oddities - if it looks important, record it."

	His eyes briefly wander toward the ruined fountain.
\end{DndReadAloud}
