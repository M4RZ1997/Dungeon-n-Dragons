\section*{\inTextCircled[10]{1}{15} Uncovered Passage}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Uncovered Passage}
{\entryfont The makeshift stairs descend only a short distance before reaching the structure uncovered beneath the plaza. The passage continues far beyond the small entry tunnel into darkness.}
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	Before you stretches a broad underground passage, far wider than any mine shaft or service tunnel. Its floor has been worn into shallow parallel grooves, while evenly spaced alcoves and faded markings line the walls. Broken carts of dark wood and rusted iron stand abandoned along the route, some still filled with stone, timber, or corroded containers.

	And some are not entirely abandoned.

	A squat automaton strains endlessly against the rear of one cart whose wheels have long since collapsed beneath it. Farther ahead, another marches steadily into a wall, pauses, turns slightly, and resumes marching into the same wall once more.
\end{DndReadAloud}
\subsection*{Examining the Passage}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Examining the Passage}
{\entryfont A character may spend a moment to inspect their surroundings:
\begin{itemize}[label={\textbf{\textbullet}}]
	\item \textbf{DC 10 Intelligence (Investigation):} The grooves in the floor were formed by countless cart wheels following roughly the same paths rather than by any fixed rail system.
	\item \textbf{DC 10 Intelligence (History):} The stonework is much older than Auchtertool and resembles ancient Dwarven construction, though its exact age is difficult to determine.
	\item \textbf{DC 12 Wisdom (Perception):} A weak but continuous airflow travels through the passage from somewhere far ahead, suggesting that the tunnel extends a considerable distance.
	\item \textbf{DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana):} Faint magical residue remains within some of the automatons and wall fixtures, but almost every active system is operating on little more than residual power.
\end{itemize}}
