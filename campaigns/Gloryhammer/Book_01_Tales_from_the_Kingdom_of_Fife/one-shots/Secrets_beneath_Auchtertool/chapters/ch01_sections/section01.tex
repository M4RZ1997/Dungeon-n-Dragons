\section*{The Ruined Plaza}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{The Ruined Plaza}
\DndDropCapLine{F}{\entryfont our days ago, the testing of a new heavy ballista ended in an unexpected strike against Auchtertool's central plaza when its experimental alignment and automatic target-acquisition systems malfunctioned. The impact shattered much of the old fountain and tore through the surrounding pavement.}

{\entryfont During clean-up and repair work two days later, workers discovered that the destruction had exposed a much older structure beneath the plaza. Further excavation revealed part of a broad underground passage, prompting all work to be suspended until a specialized expedition could investigate. Since then, crude wooden steps and supports have been fitted into the opening to allow access below.}
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	Most traces of the accident have already been cleared away, though cracked paving stones and hastily patched masonry still scar the central plaza. Where the fountain once stood yawns a broad, uneven hole surrounded by broken stone and exposed earth.

	Rough wooden steps descend through the rubble toward an ancient passage below. Beyond them, dark-gray masonry stretches into the earth, its precisely fitted stones and faint geometric markings starkly different from the foundations of Auchtertool above.

	Artificers, labour constructs, and curious onlookers watch from a cautious distance, none apparently eager to approach the edge.

	Today, that changes.
\end{DndReadAloud}
