\section*{\inTextCircled[10]{8}{15} The Exchange}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{The Exchange}
{\entryfont The streets widen into an open loading district built around broad platforms, storage bays, and old cargo lifts. This was once one of Aberdour's main transfer points, where goods arriving through the underground network were sorted before being moved deeper into the settlement.}
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	Broad stone platforms line the road ahead, bordered by loading ramps, shuttered storerooms, and ancient cranes.

	Broken carts stand where they were abandoned, some still bearing the remnants of sealed crates and trade markings. Rusted chains hang motionless from overhead gantries, while faded symbols mark different loading bays along the walls.

	Everything suggests a place once filled with noise, movement, and commerce.

	Now, only dust moves through the Exchange.
\end{DndReadAloud}
{\noindent\entryfont Characters who explore the area can find old cargo seals, trade tokens, damaged manifests, packing tools, and remnants of imported goods. Most material is worthless after centuries of decay, but the surviving markings show that Aberdour once received regular shipments from elsewhere in the underground network.}
