\section*{\inTextCircled[10]{7}{15} Maker's Quarter}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Maker's Quarter}
{\entryfont The road into Aberdour passes through a district once occupied by workshops, small foundries, and private workrooms. Unlike the industrial facilities of Auchtertool, these spaces feel surprisingly personal, filled with half-finished projects and tools left exactly where their owners abandoned them.}
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	Low workshops line the street ahead, their stone fronts broken by wide doors and shuttered windows. Inside, workbenches of different heights stand beneath racks of rusted tools, while unfinished mechanisms, articulated limbs, and tiny clockwork devices lie scattered beneath layers of dust.

	Some benches are broad and heavy, others narrow and unusually low, but all bear the marks of careful use.
\end{DndReadAloud}
{\noindent\entryfont Characters who explore the quarter find little of immediate value. Most components have deteriorated beyond use, though intact precision tools, clockwork toys, and other small curiosities may still be recovered.}
