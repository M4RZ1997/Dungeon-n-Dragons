\section*{\inTextCircled[10]{4}{15} Hidden Cache}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Hidden Cache (optional)}
{\entryfont Behind one of the old loading cranes, several narrow passages once led into a concealed chamber. Centuries of shifting stone and accumulated debris have almost completely buried their entrances, leaving the wall seemingly solid at first glance.}
\subsection*{Finding the Cache}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Finding the Cache}
{\entryfont The chamber can be discovered in several ways:
\paragraph*{Stone Resonance} B.A.S.A.L.T-5's Wardkeeper can be used to detect the hollow space beyond the wall and traces of narrow passages leading toward it.
\paragraph*{Detect Magic} L.O.R.E-3 can detect faint magical signatures behind the masonry that do not belong to the surrounding tunnel infrastructure.
\paragraph*{Examining the Stonework} S.C.O.U.T-1 can notice extremely old signs that the wall was once deliberately disturbed and excavated.

\noindent Other reasonable methods may reveal the cache as well.

The original passages are clogged with rubble and only wide enough for Small or smaller creatures. They can be reopened by digging, drilling, or breaking through the surrounding stone.}
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	Beyond the rubble lies a cramped chamber crowded with dusty shelves, sealed crates, engraved plates, and bundles of brittle notes.

	There is little here that resembles treasure.

	Whoever hid this place was concealing knowledge.
\end{DndReadAloud}
\subsection*{Stolen Research}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Stolen Research}
{\entryfont The cache once belonged to an engineer who secretly removed research and prototype components from Aberdour, hiding them here rather than transporting them through the nearby checkpoint.

The recovered material includes advanced construct schematics, prototype actuators and arcane regulators, and incomplete plans for enormous operator-controlled machines intended for excavation, siege work, and hazardous environments.

If returned to Auchtertool, the recovered research can significantly aid its artificers in constructing and improving the machines prepared for the coming war.}
