\section*{\inTextCircled[10]{10}{15} Awake the Gate}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Awake the Gate}
{\entryfont With the entrance mechanism not receiving enough energy to operate on its own, the party must restore several damaged connection and redirect what little energy remains into the locking mechanism.

Resolve the restoration in three stages. Each stage can be approached in different ways, and reasonable uses of spells, tools, or character abilities should be rewarded. A failed check does not halt progress; instead, it introduces Instability into the damaged system.}
\subsection*{Trace the Remaining Power}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Trace the Remaining Power}
{\entryfont The characters must first determine which of the ancient conduits still carry energy. A character can attempt one of the following checks:
\begin{itemize}[label=\textbf{\textbullet}]
	\item \textbf{DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) Check} to follow the remaining magical current.
	\item \textbf{DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) Check} to reconstruct the original distribution network.
	\item \textbf{DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) Check} to identify faint pulses of light, heat, or vibration within active conduits.
\end{itemize}
On a failure, the characters still locate the surviving circuit, but accidentally feed power into a dead section of the network. \textbf{Gain 1 Instability.}}
\subsection*{Reconnect the Conduits}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Reconnect the Conduit}
{\entryfont Several connections along the surviving route have deteriorated beyond normal operation. They must be repaired or bypassed before energy can reach the gates. Possible approaches include:
\begin{itemize}[label=\textbf{\textbullet}]
	\item \textbf{DC 14 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) Check} using Tinker's Tools to repair or bypass the damaged connections.
	\item \textbf{DC 14 Strength (Athletics) Check} to force a seized mechanical relay back into position.
\end{itemize}
Appropriate spells or specialized equipment, such as Mending, may grant advantage or provide another suitable solution.

On a failure, the connections are restored imperfectly. \textbf{Gain 1 Instability.}}
\subsection*{Redirect the Flow}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Redirect the Flow}
{\entryfont With the circuit restored, the remaining energy must be diverted away from the citadel’s dormant systems and concentrated into the entrance mechanism. A character can attempt one of the following:
\begin{itemize}[label=\textbf{\textbullet}]
	\item \textbf{DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) Check} to regulate the magical current directly.
	\item \textbf{DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) Check} to determine the correct sequence of regulators and switches.
	\item \textbf{DC 15 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) Check} using Tinker's Tools to manually recalibrate the distribution system.
\end{itemize}
On a failure, the energy still reaches the gates, but the damaged network struggles to contain it. \textbf{Gain 1 Instability.}}
\subsection*{Instability}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Instability}
{\entryfont Once the final stage is completed, the gates open regardless of the accumulated Instability. The total instead determines how violently the ancient system reacts as power returns.}
\subsubsection*{0 Instability - Clean Restoration}
{\entryfont Energy flows steadily through the surviving conduits. Ancient locks disengage one after another and the gates open without incident.}
\subsubsection*{1 Instability - Unstable Surge}
{\entryfont Sparks erupt from several regulators as the gates open. Creatures working directly on the mechanism must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or take 1d6 Lightning damage.}
\subsubsection*{2 Instability - System Overload}
{\entryfont The restored circuit discharges violently. Creatures near the controls must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d6 Lightning damage on a failure or half as much on a success.}
\subsubsection*{3 Instability - Emergency Discharge}
{\entryfont The gates grind open as the entire entrance network overloads. Resolve the System Overload above, after which the restored conduits burn out completely and fall dark once more.}
