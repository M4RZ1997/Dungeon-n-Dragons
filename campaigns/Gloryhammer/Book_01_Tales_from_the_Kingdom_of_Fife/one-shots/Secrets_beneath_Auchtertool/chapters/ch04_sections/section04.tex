\section*{Aftermath}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Aftermath}
{\entryfont With the core secured, the expedition retraces its path through the forgotten tunnels and eventually returns to the opening beneath Auchtertool's ruined fountain. The journey back can be summarized unless the DM wishes to revisit any unresolved location.}
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	When you finally climb through the opening beneath the plaza, the artificers waiting above immediately gather around.

	Their questions quickly fall silent when they see what you have brought back.

	What began as the accidental discovery of a forgotten passage has uncovered the remains of an entire lost kingdom - and technology Auchtertool could never have created alone.

	Before long, the recovered core and ancient research disappear into the city's workshops.

	There is a war coming, after all.
\end{DndReadAloud}
\subsection*{Effects on the Main Campaign}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Effects on the Main Campaign}
{\entryfont The discoveries made during Secrets Beneath Auchtertool influence the forces Auchtertool can later contribute to the war against Zargothrax.\\
\paragraph*{Recovered Energy Core} The type of core recovered determines the power source used for Auchtertool's great war construct.

A Spirit Energy Core produces the more powerful version, empowered by the willing spirit of the last King of Aberdour.

A Knowledge Energy Core, completed through the surviving knowledge of Aberdour's Gnomish engineers, produces a less powerful but still formidable construct.\\
\paragraph*{Hidden Cache Recovered} If the expedition discovered the Hidden Cache, Auchtertool gains access to stolen Aberdour schematics, prototype components, and research into enormous operator-controlled machines. This significantly improves the final construct built for the coming war.\\
\paragraph*{Prototype Assembly Explored} If the expedition investigated the Prototype Assembly, the artificers gain additional insight into Aberdour's experimental construct designs and the development of its ancient machines, providing a further improvement to Auchtertool's forces.}
