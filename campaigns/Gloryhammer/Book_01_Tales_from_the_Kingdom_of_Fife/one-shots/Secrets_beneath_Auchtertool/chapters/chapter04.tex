\DndDropCapLine{A}{\entryfont t the far end of the Great Plaza, broad steps ascend toward the ancient citadel of Aberdour. Even after centuries of abandonment, the structure dominates the settlement, its monumental walls built from dark stone reinforced with bands of metal. Almost every conduit and arcane fixture surrounding the entrance has gone dark, leaving only faint traces of power within the mechanisms beside the gates.}

\begin{DndReadAloud}
	The citadel rises above you, its immense gates sealed within a wall of dark stone and ancient metal.

	Faded runes, tarnished conduits, and dormant mechanisms spread across the entrance like the workings of some enormous machine. Most are lifeless, though here and there a weak pulse of light still travels through the stone before fading once more.

	The citadel is not entirely without power.

	Just very nearly.
\end{DndReadAloud}
