{\entryfont "Secrets Beneath Auchtertool" takes place shortly after the Questlords of Inverness defeated the Dread-Witch Queen of Cellardyke. In gratitude for their aid, the Mecha-King and the Robot Prince of Auchtertool pledged their allegiance to the Kingdom of Fife, promising the might of Auchtertool for the coming final battle against Zargothrax.

	With war drawing ever closer, the city's artificers have begun preparing their inventions and constructs for the conflict ahead. During the testing of new weapons of war an experimental ballista strikes the central plaza of Auchtertool. While repairing the damage around the great fountain, workers uncover something entirely unexpected: an entrance leading beneath the city.

	A team of specialized constructs is assembled to investigate and excavate the newly uncovered passage. What begins as a straightforward expedition soon leads them far beneath Auchtertool, where forgotten ruins, mysterious technologies, and secrets buried since the old days await.}
