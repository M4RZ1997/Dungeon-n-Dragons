{\entryfont\textbf{Session Length:} 3-4 hours\\
\textbf{Party Size:} 3-5 characters\\
\textbf{Party Level:} 5th level\\
\textbf{Character Creation:} Pregenerated characters recommended\\\\
Encounters throughout this adventure are designed primarily for a party of 3-5 5th-level characters and may require adjustment for groups outside this range.\\\\
"Secrets Beneath Auchtertool" is a short, exploration-focused adventure in which the players take control of a team of specialized constructs assembled for an expedition beneath the city. The provided characters represent different classes and functions within the expedition, allowing the players to experience a variety of playstyles. Custom characters can be used at the DM's discretion, though this is discouraged, as integrating individual backgrounds may prove difficult without revealing elements of the adventure beforehand.

The expedition begins after a weapons test in preparation for the coming war goes spectacularly awry. An experimental ballista damages part of Auchtertool's central plaza, and the subsequent repairs uncover a sealed passage beneath the great fountain. With neither its origin nor its destination known, the constructs are sent below to investigate what has lain forgotten beneath Auchtertool.

The adventure focuses primarily on exploration, environmental challenges, and puzzles as the expedition descends into an ancient dwarven complex. The deeper the constructs venture, however, the more their discoveries begin to challenge the established history of Fife. Advanced machinery lies dormant beneath the earth, while gnomish runes and architectural elements intermingle with dwarven craftsmanship, suggesting a connection between the two peoples during the old days that has long since been forgotten.

Although exploration and discovery form the heart of the adventure, the expedition ultimately culminates in a combat encounter, allowing the players to put the distinct capabilities of their characters to use.}
