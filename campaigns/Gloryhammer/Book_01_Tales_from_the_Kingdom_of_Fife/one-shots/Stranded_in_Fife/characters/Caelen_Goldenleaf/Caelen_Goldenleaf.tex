	The Caelthir Tribe, who call themselves the Children of the Hidden Moon, is a secluded community united by a common purpose: protecting those stranded from the Feywild and uncovering the secrets of the mysterious portals that bind the two realms. Within their hidden village of Ty Coeden, Fey Eladrin, Korreds, Satyrs, Centaurs, and other Fey creatures work together, combining their knowledge in hopes of one day reopening a stable path home. The tribe values harmony with nature, self-reliance, and the preservation of Fey traditions, while remaining wary of the wider mortal world. Every new arrival is\linebreak

}

% ADDITIONAL FEATURES & TRAITS
\AdditionalFeaturesAndTraits{
	Caelen regards every conversation as an opportunity to build understanding, whether speaking with lifelong friends or complete strangers. He practices the Bladesong each morning beneath the autumn trees surrounding Ty Coeden, treating every movement as both meditation and artistic expression. His spellbook contains careful annotations describing not only magical theory but also the emotions and intentions behind each spell, believing that a wizard's state of mind shapes their magic as much as study does. During his Bladesong, golden leaves and faint motes of amber light drift gracefully around him, making it appear as though the forest itself dances in harmony with his movements. He has an uncanny talent for remembering names, stories, and small personal details, often surprising visitors by recalling conversations years later. Even after a battle, Caelen seeks to understand what drove his opponent to fight, believing that reconciliation is the greatest victory any bladesinger can achieve.
}

% BACKGROUND
\Characterbackground{
	Caelen Goldenleaf was born in Ty Coeden, raised among elders who preserved the customs and stories of the Feywild while embracing the peace they had built within the Woods of Lomond. Drawn equally to magic and graceful swordsmanship, he devoted himself to the ancient art of the Bladesong. While many viewed it as a discipline for mastering battle, Caelen came to see it as a form of communication, believing every movement expressed intent, emotion, and respect.

	His natural gift for putting others at ease was strengthened by the Autumn Eladrin's enchanting Fey Step, inspiring him to resolve conflicts through understanding before ever drawing blood. As his reputation grew, he became one of Ty Coeden's trusted diplomats, welcoming bewildered travelers emerging from unstable portals and negotiating with outsiders willing to approach the hidden settlement in peace.

	Though fully capable of defending his home, Caelen considers violence the final failure of a conversation. Every duel is an opportunity to teach restraint, every negotiation a chance to forge lasting trust. He believes the greatest triumph of a bladesinger is not defeating an opponent, but leaving both combatants with a deeper understanding of one another.
}

\chapter*{Features, Magic Items and Spells}
\section*{Autumn Eladrin Traits}
\subsection*{Darkvision}
You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light and in darkness as if it were dim light. You discern colors in that darkness only as shades of gray.
\subsection*{Fey Ancestry}
You have advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the charmed condition on yourself.
\subsection*{Fey Step}
\textbf{\intcalcAdd{0}{\ProficiencyValue} Uses, DC: \intcalcAdd{8}{\intcalcAdd{\ProficiencyValue}{\calculateModifier{\IntelligenceScoreValue}}}}\\
As a Bonus Action, you can magically teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

Immediately after you use your Fey Step, up to two creatures of your choice that you can see within 10 feet of you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by you for 1 minute, or until you or your companions deal any damage to the creatures.
\subsection*{Keen Senses}
You have proficiency in the Perception skill.
\subsection*{Trance}
You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. You can finish a long rest in 4 hours if you spend those hours in a trancelike meditation, during which you retain consciousness.

\section*{Feats}
\subsection*{Reveling Fool}
You joined a troupe of madcap revelers and learned to take on the guise of a fool, channeling otherworldly laughter and joviality. You gain the following benefits.

\paragraph*{Fool's Mask} By donning a jovial mask and a fool's, jester's, or clown's costume, you take on a new identity that hides your own. In the guise of the fool, you can get away with minor transgressions against people in positions of authority or station.

\paragraph*{Jester's Repartee} You always have the Hideous Laughter spell prepared, and your spellcasting ability for it is Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (choose the ability when you select this feat). You can cast it once without Material components or a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast the spell using any spell slots you have.

\section*{Wizard Traits}
\subsection*{Ritual Adept}
You can cast any spell as a Ritual if that spell has the Ritual tag and the spell is in your spellbook. You needn't have the spell prepared, but you must read from the book to cast a spell in this way.
\subsection*{Arcane Recovery}
You can regain some of your magical energy by studying your spellbook. When you finish a Short Rest, you can choose expended spell slots to recover. The spell slots can have a combined level equal to no more than half your Wizard level (round up), and none of the slots can be level 6 or higher. For example, if you're a level 4 Wizard, you can recover up to two levels' worth of spell slots, regaining either one level 2 spell slot or two level 1 spell slots.

Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.
\subsection*{Scholar}
\textbf{Performance Expertise (DM decision)}\\
While studying magic, you also specialized in another field of study. Choose one of the following skills in which you have proficiency: Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, or Religion. You have Expertise in the chosen skill.
\subsection*{Bladesinger}
\subsubsection*{Bladesong}
\textbf{AC Bonus: \calculateModifier{\IntelligenceScoreValue}, \intcalcAdd{0}{\calculateModifier{\IntelligenceScoreValue}} Uses}\\
As a Bonus Action, you invoke an elven magic called the Bladesong, provided you aren't wearing armor or using a Shield.

The Bladesong lasts for 1 minute and ends early if you have the Incapacitated condition, if you don armor or a Shield, or if you use two hands to make an attack with a weapon. You can dismiss the Bladesong at any time (no action required).

While the Bladesong is active, you gain the following benefits. You can invoke the Bladesong a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest. You regain one expended use when you use Arcane Recovery.

\paragraph*{Agility} You gain a bonus to your AC equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of +1), and your Speed increases by 10 feet. In addition, you have Advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks.

\paragraph*{Bladework} Whenever you attack with a weapon with which you have proficiency, you can use your Intelligence modifier for the attack and damage rolls instead of using Strength or Dexterity.

\paragraph*{Focus} When you make a Constitution saving throw to maintain Concentration, you can add your Intelligence modifier to the total.
\subsubsection*{Training in War and Song}
\textbf{Acrobatics Proficiency}\\
You gain proficiency with all Melee Martial weapons that don't have the Two-Handed or Heavy property. You can use a Melee weapon with which you have proficiency as a Spellcasting Focus for your Wizard spells.

You also gain proficiency in one of the following skills of your choice: Acrobatics, Athletics, Performance, or Persuasion.
\subsubsection*{Memorize Spell}
Whenever you finish a Short Rest, you can study your spellbook and replace one of the level 1+ Wizard spells you have prepared for your Spellcasting feature with another level 1+ spell from the book.
\subsubsection*{Extra Attack}
You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. Moreover, you can cast one of your Wizard cantrips that has a casting time of an action in place of one of those attacks.

\subsection*{Dancing Leaf Blades}
\textit{Weapon (Shortsword), Rare (Requires Attunement)}\\
{\entryfont These elegant blades consist of two slender, leaf-shaped swords forged from silvery Feysteel. Each blade rests along the outside of the forearm within an intricate bracer of polished mithral and carved living wood, allowing the weapon to become a natural extension of the wearer's movements rather than a cumbersome armament. Fine golden filigree winds across the guards like intertwining vines, while tiny amber gemstones shimmer where the blades meet the bracers.

In the hands of a practiced Bladesinger, the paired blades appear less like weapons and more like part of an elaborate dance, tracing flowing arcs through the air as though painting music rather than delivering blows.

These magic shortswords function as a pair and must be wielded together for their magic properties to function. Attuning to the shortswords counts as being attuned to one magic item.

You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with these magic shortswords. While attuned to these weapons, you can activate the Nick mastery property in addition to a different mastery property you're using with these weapons.

\paragraph*{Finesse} When making an attack with a Finesse weapon, use your choice of your Strength or Dexterity modifier for the attack and damage rolls. You must use the same modifier for both rolls.

\paragraph*{Light} When you take the Attack action on your turn and attack with a Light weapon, you can make one extra attack as a Bonus Action later on the same turn. That extra attack must be made with a different Light weapon, and you don't add your ability modifier to the extra attack's damage unless that modifier is negative. For example, you can attack with a Shortsword in one hand and a Dagger in the other using the Attack action and a Bonus Action, but you don't add your Strength or Dexterity modifier to the damage roll of the Bonus Action unless that modifier is negative.

\paragraph*{Mastery: Nick} When you make the extra attack of the Light property, you can make it as part of the Attack action instead of as a Bonus Action. You can make this extra attack only once per turn.}
