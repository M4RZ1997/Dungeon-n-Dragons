% ADDITIONAL FEATURES & TRAITS
\AdditionalFeaturesAndTraits{
	Ronan has an uncanny sense of direction and can often retrace a path after seeing it only once. He instinctively notices broken twigs, disturbed moss, or unusual birdsong that others overlook. During quiet moments, he whittles small animal figurines from fallen branches, leaving them tucked beneath old trees for children or wandering travellers to discover. He enjoys listening to the sounds of the forest at dawn, believing each morning has its own distinct chorus. Ronan rarely raises his voice, even in tense situations, and prefers long silences over needless conversation. He carefully maintains his bow and arrows every evening, treating them with the same respect others reserve for family heirlooms. Though modest about his accomplishments, he quietly remembers every kindness shown to him and often repays it when least expected.
}

% BACKGROUND
\Characterbackground{
	Ronan was born noticeably smaller than the other foals of his herd, a difference that followed him into adulthood. While his peers excelled in contests of strength and thunderous charges through the forest, he struggled to keep pace. Rather than surrender to frustration, he devoted himself to the skills that required patience instead of power. He learned every game trail winding through the Woods of Lomond, mastered the longbow, and became an unmatched tracker whose greatest victories came before an arrow was ever loosed.

	As he matured, Ronan took on the role of a border warden for the centaur clans, quietly watching the hidden paths that led into their territory. His keen eyes and calm judgement often prevented conflict before it began, whether by guiding lost travellers away, detecting dangerous beasts, or warning his people of approaching threats.

	Although some outsiders mistake his reserved nature for timidity, those who know Ronan understand that courage is not measured by physical strength. He has earned the respect of his tribe through unwavering dedication, proving that perseverance, wisdom, and a steady hand can protect the forest just as surely as the mightiest warrior.
}

\chapter*{Features, Magic Items and Spells}

\section*{Centaur Traits}
\subsection*{Charge}
If you move at least 30 feet straight toward a target and then hit it with a melee weapon attack on the same turn, you can immediately follow that attack with a bonus action, making one attack against the target with your hooves.
\subsection*{Equine Build}
You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push or drag.

In addition, any climb that requires hands and feet is especially difficult for you because of your equine legs. When you make such a climb, each foot of movement costs you 4 extra feet instead of the normal 1 extra foot.
\subsection*{Hooves}
You have hooves that you can use to make unarmed strikes. When you hit with them, the strike deals 1d6 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike.
\subsection*{Natural Affinity}
\textbf{Medicine Proficiency}\\
Your fey connection to nature gives you an intuitive connection to the natural world and the animals within it. You therefore have proficiency in one of the following skills of your choice: Animal Handling, Medicine, Nature, or Survival.

\section*{Feats}
\subsection*{Blood Hound}
Your senses are heightened beyond those of most folk. Whether these senses were strengthened through training or the loss of other senses, or they simply matured as you did, you are a master of finding those whom you seek. You gain the following benefits.

\paragraph*{Motion Sensor} Whenever a creature that is Small or larger moves within 10 feet of you while you do not have the Unconscious condition, you immediately become aware of its presence.

\paragraph*{No Hiding} You have Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sound or smell.
\subsection*{Fighting Style: Archery}
You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls you make with Ranged weapons.

\section*{Ranger Traits}
\subsection*{Favored Enemy}
You always have the Hunter's Mark spell prepared. You can cast it twice without expending a spell slot, and you regain all expended uses of this ability when you finish a Long Rest.

The number of times you can cast the spell without a spell slot increases when you reach certain Ranger levels, as shown in the Favored Enemy column of the Ranger Features table.
\subsection*{Weapon Mastery}
\textbf{Longbow, Shortsword}\\
Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons of your choice with which you have proficiency, such as Longbows and Shortswords.

Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can change the kinds of weapons you chose. For example, you could switch to using the mastery properties of Scimitars and Longswords.
\subsubsection*{Slow (Longbow)}
If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to it, you can reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. If the creature is hit more than once by weapons that have this property, the Speed reduction doesn't exceed 10 feet.
\subsubsection*{Vex (Shortsword)}
If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to the creature, you have Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn.
\subsection*{Deft Explorer}
\textbf{Perception Expertise; Sylvan, Dwarvish}\\
Thanks to your travels, you gain the following benefits.

\paragraph*{Expertise} Choose one of your skill proficiencies with which you lack Expertise. You gain Expertise in that skill.

\paragraph*{Languages} You know two languages of your choice from the language tables in chapter 2.
\subsection*{Fighting Style}
\textbf{Archery}\\
You gain a Fighting Style feat of your choice. Instead of choosing one of those feats, you can choose the option below.
\subsection*{Monster Slayer Magic}
Starting at 3rd level, you learn an additional spell when you reach certain levels in this class, as shown in the Monster Slayer Spells table. The spell counts as a ranger spell for you, but it doesn't count against the number of ranger spells you know.
\begin{DndTable}[header=Monster Slayer Spells]{llX}
				& Ranger Level	& Spells\\
	\textbullet	& 3rd			& Protection from Evil and Good\\
	\textbullet	& 5th			& Zone of Truth\\
				& 9th			& Magic Circle\\
				& 13th			& Banishment\\
				& 17th			& Hold Monster
\end{DndTable}
\subsection*{Hunter's Sense}
At 3rd level, you gain the ability to peer at a creature and magically discern how best to hurt it. As an Action, choose one creature you can see within 60 feet of you. You immediately learn whether the creature has any damage immunities, resistances, or vulnerabilities and what they are. If the creature is hidden from divination magic, you sense that it has no damage immunities, resistances, or vulnerabilities.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses of it when you finish a long rest.
\subsection*{Slayer's Prey}
Starting at 3rd level, you can focus your ire on one foe, increasing the harm you inflict on it. As a bonus action, you designate one creature you can see within 60 feet of you as the target of this feature. The first time each turn that you hit that target with a weapon attack, it takes an extra 1d6 damage from the weapon.

This benefit lasts until you finish a short or long rest. It ends early if you designate a different creature.
\subsection*{Extra Attack}
You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.
\subsection*{Roving}
Your Speed increases by 10 feet while you aren't wearing Heavy armor. You also have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.
\subsection*{Supernatural Defense}
At 7th level, you gain extra resilience against your prey's assaults on your mind and body. Whenever the target of your Slayer's Prey forces you to make a saving throw and whenever you make an ability check to escape that target's grapple, add 1d6 to your roll.
