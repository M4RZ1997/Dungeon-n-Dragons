\section*{The Abandoned Coven}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{The Abandoned Coven}
{\entryfont Hidden within one of the older reaches of the Woods of Lomond, the characters may stumble upon a small clearing concealed between dense trees and tangled undergrowth. At its centre stands a solitary timber cottage, its walls wrapped in ivy and its roof shaded beneath the surrounding canopy.

At first glance, nothing about the building suggests long abandonment. The structure is maintained, the small herb garden outside has only begun to grow untended, and faint tracks still mark the earth around the clearing.}

\subsection*{Entering the Cottage}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Entering the Cottage}
{\entryfont The front door is unlocked and gives way with little resistance. Inside, the cottage appears less abandoned than suddenly deserted.}
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	The faint smell of dried herbs and cold woodsmoke hangs in the air. Three rocking chairs surround an extinguished fireplace, while a nearby table remains laid for three, complete with porcelain cups and tarnished silver cutlery.

	A half-finished meal has begun to spoil upon one plate. Bundles of herbs hang from the rafters, and several candles have burned down only partway before being left untouched.

	Nothing appears overturned. There are no obvious signs of struggle.

	Everything suggests that whoever lived here expected to return.
\end{DndReadAloud}
{\noindent\entryfont Nothing immediately reacts to the characters' presence. Allow them to explore freely and form their own conclusions before introducing the individual rooms and discoveries within the cottage.}

\subsection*{Exploring the Cottage}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Exploring the Cottage}
{\entryfont The cottage consists of only a handful of rooms, but almost every surface bears some trace of its former occupants. Nothing appears deliberately hidden or hastily packed away. The characters may explore the following areas in any order.}
\subsubsection*{The Kitchen}
{\entryfont Bundles of freshly dried herbs hang from the rafters above a cluttered preparation table. Clay jars contain roots, seeds, mushrooms, and other ingredients gathered throughout the Woods of Lomond, while several knives and mortars remain where they were last used. A kettle hangs above the cold hearth, its contents reduced to a dark, bitter-smelling residue.

Among the clutter, the characters may find:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Three mismatched wooden spoons, each carved with a different set of initials.
	\item A bundle of recipe cards containing ordinary stews and herbal teas alongside several recipes whose ingredients have names such as Moon's Breath or Widow's Tears.
	\item A leather-bound journal containing decades of observations about the Woods of Lomond.
\end{itemize}
\noindent The ordinary nature of many of these possessions contrasts sharply with the cottage's reputation and suggests that its inhabitants maintained something resembling a normal household.}

\subsubsection*{The Workshop}
{\entryfont The cottage's largest room serves as a combined workshop and study. Shelves are crowded with alchemical glassware, dried plants, preserved insects, strange minerals, lengths of bone, and unfinished charms. Several projects remain scattered across a central workbench as though their makers intended to continue them later.

A successful \textbf{DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) Check} reveals that several powerful enchantments were practised here, though none of the unfinished objects are presently magical.

Possible discoveries include:
\begin{itemize}
	\item A necklace of polished river stones, each marked with a different protective symbol.
	\item A bundle of coloured threads wound around small antler fragments, apparently intended for unfinished charms.
	\item A cloudy glass sphere containing a preserved dandelion seed suspended perfectly at its centre.
	\item Several pages of handwritten observations concerning unusual plants, animals, and magical phenomena found throughout the Woods of Lomond.
\end{itemize}
\noindent None of these objects possess any magical properties, though some may be valuable as curiosities or useful to anyone interested in the history of the region.}

\subsubsection*{The Sleeping Quarters}
{\entryfont Three modest beds stand along the walls of the rear room, each surrounded by distinctly different belongings. One is neatly arranged and practical, another cluttered with books and loose papers, while the third is decorated with colourful fabrics and small handmade ornaments.

Among their belongings are:
\begin{itemize}
	\item An unfinished embroidery hoop depicting three figures beneath a crescent moon.
	\item A worn deck of illustrated cards, apparently used for games rather than divination.
	\item A small collection of pressed flowers gathered from different parts of the Woods of Lomond.
\end{itemize}
\noindent Together, the belongings make it clear that three distinct individuals lived here and that the cottage was still actively occupied until very recently. Whatever caused them to leave, there is no indication that they had been preparing to do so.}

\subsection*{The Awakening}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{The Awakening}
{\entryfont The cottage is protected by a dormant defensive enchantment left behind by its former occupants. The ward does not react to simple movement or handling mundane objects, allowing the characters to explore the rooms freely at first.}
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	A faint pulse of emerald light spreads through the cottage.

	For a heartbeat, everything is still.

	Then the furniture begins to move.

	Metal scrapes against wood as suits of armor stir from their resting places. Blades wrench themselves free from the walls, hovering in the air with points turned toward you.

	Nearby, the tapestry twists violently from the wall, folding in on itself like a living thing.

	The cottage has awakened.
\end{DndReadAloud}

\subsection*{The Fate of the Coven}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{The Fate of the Coven}
{\entryfont Once the cottage has fallen silent, the characters may notice signs that its former inhabitants did not simply leave. A faint trail leads away from the clearing and into the surrounding woods. Broken branches, disturbed earth, and several dark stains remain visible despite the passing days.

After only a few minutes, it ends in a shallow hollow beneath the trees.}
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	Three bodies lie among the roots and fallen leaves. Their clothing is torn, and deep wounds cover their arms and torsos. Nearby vegetation has been crushed and scattered as though a violent struggle took place here.

	Whatever happened, the occupants of the cottage never made it far.
\end{DndReadAloud}
{\entryfont \paragraph*{Wisdom (Medicine) Check (DC 12)} The wounds were caused by claws and teeth rather than weapons. Several attacks struck from markedly different directions in quick succession, suggesting unusually fast or unpredictable predators.\\
\paragraph*{Wisdom (Survival) Check (DC 15)} Several overlapping canine tracks around the hollow can be found. Some trails appear to begin or end abruptly, with no corresponding approach or departure.}
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	The tracks eventually disappear among the undergrowth. Whatever killed the coven has already moved on.
\end{DndReadAloud}
