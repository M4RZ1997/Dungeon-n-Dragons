\clearpage\section*{The Abandoned Coven}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{The Abandoned Coven}
{\entryfont While exploring one of the oldest parts of the Woods of Lomond, the party stumbles upon a secluded woodland clearing that has long since been reclaimed by nature. Massive oaks and ancient yews encircle the area, their branches weaving together to form a dense canopy through which only a few shafts of sunlight reach the forest floor.

At the center of the clearing stands a solitary timber cottage. Thick ivy climbs its walls, moss carpets the weathered roof, and young saplings have begun growing around its foundation. Despite decades of abandonment, the structure still stands remarkably firm.}

\subsection*{Entering the Cottage}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Entering the Cottage}
{\entryfont The front door creaks open with little resistance.

Inside, everything appears untouched.

Dust covers every surface, yet the furniture is neatly arranged.

Three rocking chairs sit around an extinguished fireplace.

A table remains laid for three occupants, complete with cracked porcelain cups and tarnished silver cutlery.

Nothing suggests a struggle.

Instead, it feels as though the inhabitants simply stood up one day and never came back.}

\subsection*{Exploring the Cottage}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Exploring the Cottage}
{\entryfont Each room hints at the former occupants without fully revealing their story.}

\subsubsection*{The Kitchen}
{\entryfont Bundles of long-dried herbs still hang from the rafters.

Shelves are filled with empty glass bottles, many bearing faded handwritten labels.

A heavy iron kettle still hangs above the cold fireplace.}

\subsubsection*{The Workshop}
{\entryfont Shelves contain alchemical equipment, strange minerals, preserved insects, and jars whose contents have long since turned to dust.

Several unfinished charms lie scattered across a workbench.

A successful Arcana check reveals that powerful enchantments once permeated the room, though almost all traces have faded with time.}

\subsubsection*{The Sleeping Quarters}
{\entryfont Three modest beds occupy the room.

Each contains different personal belongings:
\begin{itemize}
	\item an unfinished embroidery hoop,
	\item a stack of handwritten notes,
	\item a weathered walking staff leaning against the wall.
\end{itemize}
Together they suggest that three distinct individuals once lived here rather than a single solitary witch.}

\subsection*{The Awakening}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{The Awakening}
{\entryfont As the party investigates further, one of the characters unknowingly disturbs a protective ward - perhaps by opening a locked cabinet, removing an old trinket, or disturbing a rune carved beneath a floorboard.}
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	A faint pulse of emerald light spreads through the cottage.

	The ancient enchantments awaken.

	Furniture rattles.

	Doors slam shut.

	The cottage itself begins to defend what was left behind.
\end{DndReadAloud}
\subsubsection*{Combat Encounter}
{\entryfont For a Level 6 party, I'd keep the encounter flavourful but relatively easy.

\textbf{Guardians of the Coven}
\begin{itemize}
	\item 2 Animated Armors
	\item 2 Flying Swords
	\item 1 Rug of Smothering
\end{itemize}
These enchanted servants were never meant to defeat powerful adventurers. Their purpose was simply to drive away curious intruders while the coven was away. Having remained dormant for decades, the magic animating them has weakened considerably.

The encounter offers a fun change of pace: instead of facing living foes, the party battles the cottage itself as armor marches from forgotten corners, blades leap from display racks, and the rug lashes out to entangle anyone standing near the hearth.}

\subsection*{Treasure}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Treasure}
{\entryfont Once the guardians have been defeated, the protective magic fades, allowing the party to safely search the cottage.

Possible discoveries include:}

\subsubsection*{Coven Ledger}
{\entryfont A leather-bound journal containing decades of observations about the Woods of Lomond.

Rather than recipes or curses, it records seasonal changes, rare creatures, unusual weather, and sightings of magical phenomena. The final entries stop abruptly, offering no explanation for the coven's disappearance.}
