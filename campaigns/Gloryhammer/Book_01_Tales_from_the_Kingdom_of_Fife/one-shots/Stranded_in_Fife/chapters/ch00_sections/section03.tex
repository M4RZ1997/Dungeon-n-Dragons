\section*{\LARGE Adventure Background}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Adventure Background}
{\entryfont The closing of the Feywild portals marks a turning point for Ty Coeden. What had once been an ever-present uncertainty has disappeared literally overnight, leaving the Caelthir with a future that is safer, but also far less certain. The events of the past few days have forced the village to confront questions that had long been postponed, while subtle consequences of the Questlords' actions are already beginning to take shape elsewhere in the Woods of Lomond.}
\subsection*{The Caelthir's New Purpose}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{The Caelthir's New Purpose}
{\entryfont Only a few days have passed since the portals throughout the Woods of Lomond were permanently closed. Their disappearance has not yet diminished the lingering influence of the Feywild upon the region; strange plants, unusual creatures, and traces of otherworldly magic remain a familiar part of the forest. What has changed is the certainty that no new portals will appear. For generations, the Caelthir watched for these rifts, protected the surrounding woods from dangerous creatures that emerged through them, and aided those who became stranded far from the Feywild. With that responsibility suddenly gone, a defining part of life in Ty Coeden has vanished with it.}
\subsection*{An Uncertain Future}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{An Uncertain Future}
{\entryfont The end of the portals has left the Caelthir divided over what should come next. Some believe Ty Coeden should preserve the isolation that has protected the tribe for generations, while others see an opportunity to form closer ties with the peoples of Fife. Many remain caught between these positions, unwilling to abandon their traditions yet uncertain whether continuing as before is even possible. With no practical means of returning to the Feywild, the Caelthir must now decide whether the Material Plane is merely the place where they remain stranded - or the place they are finally willing to call home.}
\subsection*{Beyond Ty Coeden}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Beyond Ty Coeden}
{\entryfont Although Ty Coeden has remained deliberately secluded, the Caelthir have never been entirely alone within the Woods of Lomond. Hunters, lumberjacks, travellers, and other inhabitants of Fife have long passed through the surrounding region, usually unaware of just how close they came to the hidden settlement. Meaningful contact between these groups has remained rare, but the disappearance of the portals has given such relationships new importance. If the Caelthir intend to build a lasting future in Fife, the people living beyond their hidden paths may no longer be neighbours they can simply ignore.}
\subsection*{The Surviving Pups}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{The Surviving Pups}
{\entryfont Questlords have survived within the Woods of Lomond and are beginning to transform into Zircon Foxes. Their presence has not yet been recognized, though the behaviour of wildlife near Loch Leven is already beginning to reflect the arrival of unusually dangerous predators.\\\\
The characters should neither encounter nor conclusively identify the Zircon Foxes during this adventure. Any signs of their presence should remain subtle and unexplained, serving only to create a growing sense that something is changing within the forest. Their emergence is reserved for the final cliffhanger of Stranded in Fife, where the predators first turn their attention toward Ty Coeden.}
