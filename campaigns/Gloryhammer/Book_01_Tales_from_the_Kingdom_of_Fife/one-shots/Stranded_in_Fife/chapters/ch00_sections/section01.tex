\section*{\LARGE Campaign Context}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Campaign Context}
{\entryfont "Stranded in Fife" takes place shortly after the Questlords of Inverness departed Ty Coeden. During their journey through the Woods of Lomond, the adventurers uncovered the cause behind the unstable portals to the Feywild that had plagued the region for generations.

Deep within Lochleven Castle, they successfully completed a ritual that permanently closed the portals. However, the ancient castle collapsed during the ritual, claiming the lives of all who remained within.

Only a few days have passed since these events, and their consequences are only beginning to settle over Ty Coeden. For generations, the Caelthir dedicated themselves to understanding the portals, protecting the surrounding woods from what emerged through them, and aiding those who became stranded far from the Feywild.

Now, that purpose is suddenly gone.

With no practical way to return to the realm of their ancestors, the inhabitants of Ty Coeden face an uncertain future. For the first time in generations, they must look beyond the hidden boundaries of their village and ask what place they wish to claim within the Kingdom of Fife.}

\subsection*{Adventure Requirements}\stepcounter{subsection}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Adventure Requirements}
{\entryfont "Stranded in Fife" follows a specific outcome of the events in the Woods of Lomond. The one-shot should only be played if the Questlords of Inverness:
\begin{itemize}
	\item successfully completed the ritual within Lochleven Castle, permanently closing the unstable portals to the Feywild
	\item left the warlock responsible for disrupting the portals within the castle, where he perished during its collapse
	\item killed the adult Cobalt Foxes while allowing their pups to escape.
\end{itemize}}
