\section*{\LARGE Adventure Overview}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Adventure Overview}
{\entryfont\textbf{Session Length:} 3-4 hours\\
\textbf{Party Size:} 3-5 characters\\
\textbf{Party Level:} 5th Level\\
\textbf{Character Creation:} Pregenerated characters recommended\\\\
Encounter throughout this adventure are designed primarily for a party of 3-5 5th-level characters and may require adjustment for groups outside this range.

\noindent The adventure begins in Ty Coeden, where the Caelthir gather to discuss their uncertain future following the permanent closure of the Feywild portals. With returning to the Feywild no longer a practical possibility, the characters are tasked with taking the tribe's first cautious steps toward establishing a place within the Kingdom of Fife.

Their journey takes them through the Woods of Lomond and toward Loch Leven, where they encounter a nearby human lumber camp, local hunters, and the deserted nest of a long-vanished hag coven. Upon returning to Ty Coeden, the inhabitants celebrate the Festival of New Roots, only for the adventure to close with the first ominous signs that the surviving Cobalt Fox pups have become something far more dangerous.

"Stranded in Fife" places a strong emphasis on social interaction, diplomacy, and exploration rather than combat. The adventure provides the characters with opportunities to interact with the inhabitants of the Woods of Lomond, explore the changing circumstances surrounding Ty Coeden, and influence how the Caelthir begin to approach their uncertain future within the Kingdom of Fife.}
