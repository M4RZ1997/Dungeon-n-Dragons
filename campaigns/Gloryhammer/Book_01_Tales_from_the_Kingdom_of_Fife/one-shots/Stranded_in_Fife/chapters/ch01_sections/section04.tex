\section*{A Quiet Ending}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{A Quiet Ending}
{\entryfont As the gathering begins to disperse, allow the scene to end on a quieter and more personal note. The debate has focused on the future of the Caelthir as a people; this final moment instead reflects what the closing of the portals means to those whose lives were shaped by watching for them.

An elderly scout remains seated for a moment longer than the others before speaking softly:}
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	"I woke before dawn today."

	"For more than two hundred years, the first thing I did every morning was look toward the treeline and search for the light of another portal. Sometimes I found one. Most days I did not. But I always looked."

	He lowers his gaze, a faint, uncertain smile crossing his face.

	"This morning... I realised I no longer had a reason."

	For a moment, nobody answers.

	One by one, the remaining members of the gathering rise and leave the hall. Beyond its doors, Ty Coeden carries on beneath the ancient trees - beginning the first ordinary day its people have known in generations.
\end{DndReadAloud}
