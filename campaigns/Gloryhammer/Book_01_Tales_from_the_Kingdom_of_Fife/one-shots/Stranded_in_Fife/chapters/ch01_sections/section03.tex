\section*{The Decision}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{The Decision}
{\entryfont The council does not reach a final agreement on what Ty Coeden's future should become. Too many questions remain unanswered, and even those who share the same concerns often disagree on how the Caelthir should respond to them. Rather than forcing an irreversible choice, the gathering settles upon a cautious compromise: before deciding whether to remain hidden, open themselves to the outside world, or seek some path between the two, they should first learn more about the people and circumstances surrounding them.

A senior member of the council brings the discussion to a close:}
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	“"We have spent long enough debating a world most of us scarcely know. Before we decide whether to welcome it or hide from it, perhaps we should first understand it."

	"Walk beyond the paths we know. Speak with those who dwell near these woods, watch how they live, and learn what place there may be for us among them. Return not with promises or treaties, but with knowledge. Then we may speak again of what Ty Coeden should become."
\end{DndReadAloud}
{\noindent\entryfont The characters are therefore encouraged to venture beyond the immediate boundaries of Ty Coeden and explore the surrounding Woods of Lomond and the region near Loch Leven. They are not given a strict route or a list of objectives. Instead, they are asked to observe, interact where appropriate, and return with a better understanding of the opportunities and difficulties that closer contact with the people of Fife might bring.

The exact tone of this decision may reflect the preceding debate. If the characters strongly advocated greater openness, the journey can be framed as a cautious first step toward contact. If they favored continued isolation, it may instead be presented as reconnaissance intended to inform future decisions. In either case, the practical outcome remains the same and leads directly into Finding One's Place, where the encounters beyond Ty Coeden may occur in any order.}
