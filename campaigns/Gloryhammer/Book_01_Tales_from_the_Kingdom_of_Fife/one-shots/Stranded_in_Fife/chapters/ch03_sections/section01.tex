\section*{Preparations}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Preparations}
{\entryfont Throughout the village, everyone contributes in their own way.
\begin{itemize}
	\item Hunters prepare a communal feast from the day's catch.
	\item Artisans decorate the central square with ribbons, carved lanterns and fresh flowers gathered from the forest.
	\item Musicians tune long-forgotten instruments that had rarely been played outside seasonal ceremonies.
	\item Children weave flower crowns and eagerly help wherever they can, enjoying the unusual excitement.
\end{itemize}
For perhaps the first time in centuries, no one has to interrupt the festivities to answer a scout's report of another portal opening somewhere within the woods.}
