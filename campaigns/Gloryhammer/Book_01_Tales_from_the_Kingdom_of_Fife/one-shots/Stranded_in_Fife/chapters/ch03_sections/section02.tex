\section*{A Time to Remember}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{A Time to Remember}
{\entryfont As dusk settles over Ty Coeden, the entire village gathers beneath the great trees.

The council briefly addresses the assembled Caelthir.

They honour those who gave their lives protecting the village over the generations, as well as those who recently perished beneath Loch Leven while ensuring that the portals would never again threaten either world.

Rather than mourning, the gathering celebrates their sacrifice by acknowledging that their centuries of vigilance achieved its purpose.

The players are invited to share stories from their journey, recounting their meeting with the lumber camp and their discoveries within the woods. Their experiences reassure many that life beyond isolation may not be as frightening as some had feared.}
