\section*{The Planting}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{The Planting}
{\entryfont At the centre of the village, a young oak sapling has been prepared.

The council explains that it will remain where it is planted, growing alongside future generations of Caelthir who may never witness a Feywild portal.

Each member of the party is invited to place a handful of soil around its roots or offer a few words if they wish. No speeches are required - some characters may simply stand in silence.

The moment symbolizes a simple truth:

The Caelthir can no longer look toward another world.

It is time to grow in this one.}
