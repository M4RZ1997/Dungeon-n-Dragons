\section*{The Celebration}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{The Celebration}
{\entryfont The solemnity soon gives way to music, dancing and laughter.

The characters have one final opportunity to simply enjoy the company of the village. They might dance alongside friends and family, exchange stories with elders, sample unfamiliar dishes, teach children games learned elsewhere, or simply watch the celebration from beneath the trees.

There is no task to complete and no decision left to make.

For tonight, uncertainty can wait.}
