\section*{The Celebration}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{The Celebration}
{\entryfont The solemn mood soon gives way to music, dancing and laughter.

The evening offers the players one final opportunity to simply enjoy the company of the village.

Perhaps they:
\begin{itemize}
	\item dance alongside friends and family,
	\item exchange stories with elders,
	\item teach children games they learned elsewhere,
	\item sample new recipes inspired by human ingredients,
	\item or simply watch the celebration from beneath the trees.
\end{itemize}
There is no urgency.

No looming mission.

Just one peaceful evening earned after centuries of uncertainty.}
