\DndDropCapLine{F}{\entryfont or generations, the Caelthir Tribe has lived hidden beneath the ancient boughs of the Woods of Lomond, watching over the unstable portals that bound their secluded home to the Feywild. Now, for the first time in centuries, those portals have fallen silent. With their old purpose suddenly gone and no practical path back to the realm of their ancestors, the people of Ty Coeden must turn their eyes toward the world around them. Now, the Caelthir are about to take the first tentative steps toward a new future within the Kingdom of Fife.}
