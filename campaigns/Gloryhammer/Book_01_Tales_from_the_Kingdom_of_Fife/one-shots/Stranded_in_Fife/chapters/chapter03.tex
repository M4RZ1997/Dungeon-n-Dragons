{\entryfont The council decides that the day should not simply fade into another ordinary evening. Instead, preparations begin for the first celebration of a new era - one no longer defined by waiting for another Feywild portal to appear.

The festival does not settle the question of Ty Coeden's future. It marks only the first step toward discovering what that future might become.}
