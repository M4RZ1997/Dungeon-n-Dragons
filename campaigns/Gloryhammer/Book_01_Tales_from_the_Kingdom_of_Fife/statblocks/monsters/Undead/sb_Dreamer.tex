	\DndMonsterSection{Traits}
    \DndMonsterAction{Nightmarish Fortitude}
	If damage reduces the Dreamer to 0 Hit Points, it makes a Constitution saving throw (DC 5 plus the damage taken) unless the damage is Radiant or from a Critical Hit. On a successful save, the Dreamer drops to 1 Hit Point instead and emits a nightmarish shriek. Each creature within 10 feet must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or have Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.
	
	\DndMonsterAction{Shrouded in Nightmares}
	Creatures that start their turn within 5 feet of the Dreamer must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be surrounded by waking nightmares slightly clouding their vision. Until the start of their next turn, the creature has Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and Opportunity Attacks.
