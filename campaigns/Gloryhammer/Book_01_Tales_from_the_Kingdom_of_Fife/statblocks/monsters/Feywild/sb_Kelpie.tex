	\DndMonsterSection{Traits}
	\DndMonsterAction{Amphibious}
	The Kelpie can breath air and water.
	
	\DndMonsterAction{Shapechanger}
	The Kelpie can appear as a magnificent horse or reveal their gaunt, waterlogged fey form.
	
	\DndMonsterAction{Keen Hearing and Smell}
	The Kelpie has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) Checks that rely on hearing or smell.
	
	\DndMonsterAction{Adhesive Hide}
	Any creature that touches the Kelpie (or hits it with a melee attack within 5 feet while it is in its horse form) must succeed on a DC 14 Strength Save or become grappled (escape DC 14). While grappled this way, the creature is restrained.
