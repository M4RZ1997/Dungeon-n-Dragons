	\DndMonsterSection{Traits}
    \DndMonsterAction{Elemental Nature}
	The Firegeist doesn't require air, food, drink, or sleep.
	
    \DndMonsterAction{Hide by Firelight}
	The Firegeist has Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) Checks made to hide in fire or in areas lit by non-magical fire.
	
    \DndMonsterAction{Magical Light Sensitivity}
	While in an area of light created by a spell or other magical effect, the Firegeist has Disadvantage on Attack Rolls and Ability Checks.
	
    \DndMonsterAction{Variable Illumination}
	The Firegeist sheds dim light in a 5- to 20-foot radius. It can alter the radius as a Bonus Action.
	
    \DndMonsterAction{Water Susceptibility}
	For every 5 feet the Firegeist moves in water, or for every gallon of water splashed on it, it takes 1 Cold damage.
