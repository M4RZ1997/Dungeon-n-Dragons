	\DndMonsterSection{Traits}
    \DndMonsterAction{Fire Aura}
	At the end of each of the elemental's turns, each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the elemental takes \DndDice{1d10} Fire damage. Creatures and flammable objects in the Emanation start burning.
	
    \DndMonsterAction{Fire Form}
	The elemental can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch without expending extra movement to do so, and it can enter a creature's space and stop there. The first time it enters a creature's space on a turn, that creature takes \DndDice{1d10} Fire damage.
	
    \DndMonsterAction{Illumination}
	The elemental sheds Bright Light in a 30-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 30 feet.
	
    \DndMonsterAction{Water Susceptibility}
	The elemental takes \DndDice{1d6} Cold damage for every 5 feet the elemental moves in water or for every gallon of water splashed on it.
