\chapter*{Features, Magic Items and Spells}

\section*{Human Traits}
\subsection*{Resourceful}
You gain Heroic Inspiration whenever you finish a Long Rest.
\subsection*{Skillful}
\textbf{Perception Proficiency}\\
You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice.
\subsection*{Versatile}
\textbf{Tavern Brawler}\\
You gain an Origin feat of your choice.

\section*{Feats}
\subsection*{Tavern Brawler}
You gain the following benefits.
\subparagraph*{Enhanced Unarmed Strike} When you hit with your Unarmed Strike and deal damage, you can deal Bludgeoning damage equal to 1d4 plus your Strength modifier instead of the normal damage of Unarmed Strike.
\subparagraph*{Damage Rerolls} Whenever you roll a damage die for your Unarmed Strike, you can reroll the die if it rolls a 1, and you must use the new roll.
\subparagraph*{Improvised Weaponry} You have proficiency with improvised weapons.
\subparagraph*{Push} When you hit a creature with an Unarmed Strike as part of the Attack action on your turn, you can deal damage to the target and also push it 5 feet away from you. You can use this benefit only once per turn.
\subsection*{Tough}
Your Hit Point maximum increases by an amount equal to twice your character level when you gain this feat. Whenever you gain a character level thereafter, your Hit Point maximum increases by an additional 2 Hit Points.

\section*{Barbarian Traits}
\subsection*{Rage}
\textbf{Uses: 4, Rage Damage: +3}\\
You can imbue yourself with a primal power called Rage, a force that grants you extraordinary might and resilience. You can enter it as a Bonus Action if you aren't wearing Heavy armor.
You regain one expended use when you finish a Short Rest, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
While active, your Rage follows the rules below.
\subparagraph*{Damage Resistance} You have Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
\subparagraph*{Rage Damage} When you make an attack using Strength - with either a weapon or an Unarmed Strike - and deal damage to the target, you gain a bonus to the damage that increases as you gain levels as a Barbarian, as shown in the Rage Damage column of the Barbarian Features table.
\subparagraph*{Strength Advantage} You have Advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws.
\subparagraph*{No Concentration or Spells} You can't maintain Concentration, and you can't cast spells.
\subparagraph*{Duration} The Rage lasts until the end of your next turn, and it ends early if you don Heavy armor or have the Incapacitated condition. If your Rage is still active on your next turn, you can extend the Rage for another round by doing one of the following:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Make an attack roll against an enemy.
	\item Force an enemy to make a saving throw.
	\item Take a Bonus Action to extend your Rage.
\end{itemize}
Each time the Rage is extended, it lasts until the end of your next turn. You can maintain a Rage for up to 10 minutes.
\subsection*{Unarmored Defense}
\textbf{AC = \intcalcAdd{10}{\intcalcAdd{\calculateModifier{\DexterityScoreValue}}{\calculateModifier{\ConstitutionScoreValue}}}}\\
While you aren't wearing any armor, your base Armor Class equals 10 plus your Dexterity and Constitution modifiers. You can use a Shield and still gain this benefit.
\subsection*{Weapon Mastery}
\textbf{Known Weapon Masteries: 3}\\
Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of Simple or Martial Melee weapons of your choice, such as Greataxes and Handaxes. Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can practice weapon drills and change one of those weapon choices.

When you reach certain Barbarian levels, you gain the ability to use the mastery properties of more kinds of weapons, as shown in the Weapon Mastery column of the Barbarian Features table.
\subsubsection*{Battleaxe - Topple}
\textbf{DC (Strength): \intcalcAdd{8}{\intcalcAdd{\calculateModifier{\StrengthScoreValue}}{\ProficiencyValue}}}\\
If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can force the creature to make a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 plus the ability modifier used to make the attack roll and your Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, the creature has the Prone condition.
\subsubsection*{Greataxe - Cleave}
If you hit a creature with a melee attack roll using this weapon, you can make a melee attack roll with the weapon against a second creature within 5 feet of the first that is also within your reach. On a hit the second creature takes the weapon's damage, but don't add your ability modifier to that damage unless that modifier is negative. You can make this extra attack only once per turn.
\subsubsection*{Handaxe - Vex}
If you hit a creature with this weapon and deal damage to the creature, you have Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn.
\subsection*{Danger Sense}
You gain an uncanny sense of when things aren't as they should be, giving you an edge when you dodge perils. You have Advantage on Dexterity saving throws unless you have the Incapacitated condition.
\subsection*{Reckless Attack}
You can throw aside all concern for defense to attack with increased ferocity. When you make your first attack roll on your turn, you can decide to attack recklessly. Doing so gives you Advantage on attack rolls using Strength until the start of your next turn, but attack rolls against you have Advantage during that time.
\subsection*{Path of the Berserker}
\subsubsection*{Frenzy}
\textbf{Additional Damage: 3d6}\\
If you use Reckless Attack while your Rage is active you deal extra damage to the first target you hit on your turn with a Strength-based attack. To determine the extra damage, roll a number of d6s equal to your Rage Damage bonus, and add them together. The damage has the same type as the weapon or Unarmed Strike used for the attack.
\subsubsection*{Mindless Rage}
You have Immunity to the Charmed and Frightened conditions while your Rage is active. If you're Charmed or Frightened when you enter your Rage, the condition ends on you.
\subsection*{Primal Knowledge}
\textbf{Survival Proficiency}\\
You gain proficiency in another skill of your choice from the skill list available to Barbarians at level 1.

In addition, while your Rage is active, you can channel primal power when you attempt certain tasks; whenever you make an ability check using one of the following skills, you can make it as a Strength check even if it normally uses a different ability: Acrobatics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth, Survival. When you use this ability, your Strength represents primal power coursing though you, honing your agility, bearing, and senses.
\subsection*{Extra Attack}
You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.
\subsection*{Fast Movement}
Your speed increases by 10 feet while you aren't wearing Heavy armor.
\subsection*{Feral instinct}
Your instincts are so honed that you have Advantage on Initiative rolls.
\subsection*{Instinctive Pounce}
As part of the Bonus Action you take to enter your Rage, you can move up to half your Speed.
\subsection*{Brutal Strike}
If you use Reckless Attack, you can forgo any Advantage on one Strength-based attack roll of your choice on your turn. The chosen attack roll mustn't have Disadvantage. If the chosen attack roll hits, the target takes an extra 1d10 damage of the same type dealt by the weapon or Unarmed Strike, and you can cause a Brutal Strike effect of your choice. You have the following effect options.
\subparagraph*{Forceful Blow} The target is pushed 15 feet straight away from you. You can then move up to half your Speed straight toward the target without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
\subparagraph*{Hamstring Blow} The target's Speed is reduced by 15 feet until the start of your next turn. A target can be affected by only one Hamstring blow at a time - the most recent one.
