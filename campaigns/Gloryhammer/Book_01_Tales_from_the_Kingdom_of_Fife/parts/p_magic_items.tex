\subsection*{Arcane Insulator}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Arcane Insulator}\label{mi:ArcaneInsulator}
\textit{Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)}\\
{\entryfont This compact artificer's device consists of several copper coils, articulated metal plates, and small arcane conductors. When attached near a source of unstable magic, the device projects a thin insulating field intended to shield magical energy from outside interference. The device has 10 uses.

\subparagraph*{Arcane Insulation} Once per turn, whenever the \hyperref[mi:EmberboundFragment]{\LinkFont{Emberbound Fragment}} would reduce Fire Damage you deal, the Arcane Insulator activates. The fragment's penalty is ignored for half of the Fire Damage Dice rolled, rounded up. The Arcane Insulator then expends one use.

\subparagraph*{Catastrophic Failure} When the Arcane Insulator expends its final use, the device overloads and explodes. You must make a \textbf{DC 13 Dexterity Saving Throw}, taking 2d6 Fire Damage and 2d6 Force Damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. The Arcane Insulator is destroyed.}

\subsection*{Feyfire Kindling}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Feyfire Kindling}\label{mi:FeyfireKindling}
\textit{Wondrous Item, Uncommon, Consumable}\\
{\entryfont A small bundle of silvery twigs, dried leaves, and aromatic resin gathered from plants touched by the Feywild. When ignited, the kindling burns with shifting crimson and golden flames that give off little smoke.

As an Action, you can ignite the kindling and expose the \hyperref[mi:EmberboundFragment]{\LinkFont{Emberbound Fragment}} to its flames. The fragment consumes the magical fire, destroying the kindling and becoming temporarily satiated. For the next 24 hours, the penalties to Attack Rolls and Fire Damage Dice imposed by the Emberbound Fragment are suppressed.}

\subsection*{Overgrown Barkshield}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Overgrown Barkshield}\label{mi:OvergrownBarkshield}

\indent\textit{Shield, uncommon (requires attunement)}\\
{\entryfont This crude shield made of bark and wood teems with life as if it were still part of a tree. The smell of sap and various fragrant flowers perfumes the air around you.

\paragraph*{Bark Armour} As an action, you can place the shield on your torso and speak the command word, causing the wood and vines to wrap around your figure. For one hour, or until you end this effect as an action, you are unable to hold a shield and you gain the following benefits:
\begin{itemize}
	\item You gain 1d8 temporary hit points.
	\item You gain the benefits of the barkskin spell (no concentration required).
	\item You have advantage on saving throws made to resist being poisoned.
\end{itemize}

When this effect ends, the barkshield falls from its place on your torso, fading to a lifeless shade of grey. This property can't be used again until you finish a long rest when the shield blooms with life once more.

\paragraph*{Vines and Thorns} While attuned to this shield, you can use an action to animate one of the shield's thorny vines and make a melee weapon attack (+5 to hit, reach 30 ft.) against one target. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 piercing damage, and, if the target is a Large or smaller creature, or an object that weighs less than 500 pounds, you pull it up to 10 feet closer to you.}
