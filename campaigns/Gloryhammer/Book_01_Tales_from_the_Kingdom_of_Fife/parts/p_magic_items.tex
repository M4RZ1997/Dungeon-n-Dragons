\section*{Broken Armour Piece}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Broken Armour Piece}\label{mi:Broken Armour Piece}
\textit{Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement))}\\
{\entryfont This jagged shard of scorched plate armour is cold to the touch, its blackened metal veined with dull crimson cracks that never emit warmth. Though broken and incomplete, it clings unnaturally to those who handle it.

When touching the fragment, the creature immediately becomes attuned to it. This attunement ignores the normal time required and does not require any consent.

\paragraph*{Curse} This item is cursed. Attuning to it curses you until you are targeted by a Remove Curse spell or similar magic. As long as you remain cursed, you are unwilling to part with the fragment. If it leaves your possession, it magically reappears in your pocket, pack, or among your belongings at the next dawn.

The curse deepens the longer you remain attuned to the fragment. The later stages of this curse cannot be detected, identified, or revealed by spells such as identify, divination magic, experimentation, or any other means before they manifest. When a new stage takes effect, its effects become immediately obvious to the attuned creature.

\subparagraph*{Smothered Embers (Stage 1)} After four days attuned to the fragment, whenever you make an Attack Roll for a Spell or Weapon Attack that deals Fire damage, you take a -1 penalty to the attack roll. In addition, whenever you roll Fire Damage, reduce the result of each individual Damage Die by 1 (minimum of 1 per die).

\subparagraph*{Fading Inferno (Stage 2)} After seven days attuned to the fragment, the penalty to Attack Rolls becomes -2, and each individual Fire Damage Die is reduced by 2 (minimum of 1 per die).

\subparagraph*{Ashen Ruin (Stage 3)} After nine days attuned to the fragment, you have Disadvantage on Attack Rolls for Spells or Weapon Attacks that deal Fire Damage instead of taking the previous attack roll penalty. Fire Damage Dice remain reduced as in Stage 2.}

\section*{\hspace*{-0.1em}Overgrown Barkshield}\stepcounter{section}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Overgrown Barkshield}\label{mi:OvergrownBarkshield}

\indent\textit{Shield, uncommon (requires attunement)}\\
{\entryfont This crude shield made of bark and wood teems with life as if it were still part of a tree. The smell of sap and various fragrant flowers perfumes the air around you.

\paragraph*{Bark Armour} As an action, you can place the shield on your torso and speak the command word, causing the wood and vines to wrap around your figure. For one hour, or until you end this effect as an action, you are unable to hold a shield and you gain the following benefits:
\begin{itemize}
	\item You gain 1d8 temporary hit points.
	\item You gain the benefits of the barkskin spell (no concentration required).
	\item You have advantage on saving throws made to resist being poisoned.
\end{itemize}

When this effect ends, the barkshield falls from its place on your torso, fading to a lifeless shade of grey. This property can't be used again until you finish a long rest when the shield blooms with life once more.

\paragraph*{Vines and Thorns} While attuned to this shield, you can use an action to animate one of the shield's thorny vines and make a melee weapon attack (+5 to hit, reach 30 ft.) against one target. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 piercing damage, and, if the target is a Large or smaller creature, or an object that weighs less than 500 pounds, you pull it up to 10 feet closer to you.}
