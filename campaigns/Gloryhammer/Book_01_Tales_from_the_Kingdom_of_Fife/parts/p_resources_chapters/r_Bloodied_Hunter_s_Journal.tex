\chapter*{Bloodied Hunter's Journal}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Bloodied Hunter's Journal}\label{resource:BloodiedHuntersJournal}
\begin{LeatherBoundJournal}[bloodied, font=\notefont, fontsize=12pt]
{\entryfont\textbf{3rd September, 992 AD}}\\
Dead stag near the eastern trail. Barely eaten. Strange wounds and deep marks in the earth. Something large is roaming the woods.\\\vspace*{\fontdimen6\font}

{\entryfont\textbf{6th September, 992 AD}}\\
More carcasses. Fergus saw a unicorn near the creek - thin, filthy, staggering. Could be sick. Could be something worse. People are frightened.\\\vspace*{\fontdimen6\font}

{\entryfont\textbf{8th September, 992 AD}}\\
Another sighting near the farms. Snapping at nothing, scraping its horn against the trees. Everyone speaks of the same beast now.

Some say it's rabid. Others say undead. Nobody wants to enter the woods.\\\vspace*{\fontdimen6\font}

{\entryfont\textbf{10th September, 992 AD}}\\
Ewan left for Prince Angus' wedding tourney in Dundee. Couldn't have chosen a worse time.

People are angry. Heard someone call him \textit{"Dundee's protector"} today. Another said prestige mattered more to him than Dunkeld.

Fear makes people cruel. Still... I wish he were here.\\\vspace*{\fontdimen6\font}

{\entryfont\textbf{11th September, 992 AD}}\\
Another carcass. Closer this time. Five of us are going after the beast tomorrow. If Ewan isn't here to deal with it, someone must.\\\vspace*{\fontdimen6\font}

\JournalPageBreak
{\entryfont\textbf{12th September, 992 AD}}\\
{\entryfont\fontsize{9}{10.8}\selectfont\textbf{1st Day of the Hunt}}\\
Tracks lead north. Found another carcass. Barely touched. Whatever this thing is, it is not killing for food.\\\vspace*{\fontdimen6\font}

{\entryfont\textbf{13th September, 992 AD}}\\
{\entryfont\fontsize{9}{10.8}\selectfont\textbf{2nd Day of the Hunt}}\\
Lost the tracks twice. Found them again near the old hunting grounds. Calum wants to turn back. Says something feels wrong.

We came too far for that.\\\vspace*{\fontdimen6\font}

{\entryfont\textbf{14th September, 992 AD}}\\
{\entryfont\fontsize{9}{10.8}\selectfont\textbf{3rd Day of the Hunt}}\\
Heard it during the night. Hooves between the trees.

No one slept. Found tracks around the camp this morning. The beast knows we're here.\\\vspace*{\fontdimen6\font}

{\entryfont\textbf{15th September, 992 AD}}\\
{\entryfont\fontsize{9}{10.8}\selectfont\textbf{4th Day of the Hunt}}\\
Close now. Very close. We saved our arrows for this. For whatever it truly is.

Heard it again before dawn. We're going after it.
\end{LeatherBoundJournal}
