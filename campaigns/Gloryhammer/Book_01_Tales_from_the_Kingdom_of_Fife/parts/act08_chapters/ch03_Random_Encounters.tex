\section*{Moor and Swamp}\phantomsection\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Moor and Swamp}
\subsection*{Sinking Caravan}
\subsubsection*{Encounter Type}
Environmental Hazard with optional combat complication\\
Recommended Level: 3 - 5\\
Difficulty: Moderate (resource-draining if mishandled)
\subsubsection*{Setup}
As the party travels the main trade path skirting the swamps near Paisley, they hear panicked shouting ahead, followed by the frantic whinnying of horses and the splintering creak of wood under strain.

Rounding a bend reveals a small two-wagon caravan half-sunken into a stretch of deceptively soft ground. What appeared to be solid road has liquefied into a deep mire.

Two draft horses are already chest-deep in mud, thrashing in blind panic. The more they struggle, the faster they sink.

A third horse has broken free of its harness and stands nearby, trembling and wild-eyed.

Three merchants attempt to free the animals with ropes - but every step they take risks pulling themselves into the bog.
\begin{DndReadAloud}
	Ahead, the road dissolves into a churning expanse of black-brown sludge. A wagon leans at a sickening angle, one wheel already swallowed.

	Two massive draft horses scream in panic, their flanks heaving as mud climbs higher along their sides. Each frantic movement drags them deeper.

	A merchant coated in filth shouts, "They're going under! Gods help us, they're going under!"
\end{DndReadAloud}

\subsubsection*{Mechanics}
{\entryfont This is not normal mud — it behaves like semi-liquid peat bog.
\begin{itemize}
	\item Area: 30 ft diameter stretch of unstable ground.
	\item Difficult Terrain.
	\item Any creature entering must succeed on a DC 14 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check.
	\begin{itemize}
		\item On a failure: restrained as they sink 1 ft per round.
		\item On a failure by 5+: prone and restrained.
	\end{itemize}
	\item Each round a restrained creature sinks 1 additional foot unless freed.
	\item At 4 feet submerged: suffocation risk begins.
\end{itemize}
The horses will fully submerge in 4 rounds if not helped.}

\subsubsection*{Possible Solutions}
Encourage creative problem-solving. Examples:
\paragraph*{Physical Rescue}
\begin{itemize}
	\item Use ropes, pitons, and coordinated pulls (Group DC 15 Strength check).
	\item Calm the horses first (DC 13 Animal Handling reduces DCs by 2).
	\item Create a stable path using planks, wagon boards, or debris.
\end{itemize}
\paragraph*{Magical Assistance}
\begin{itemize}
	\item Mold Earth to firm ground.
	\item Shape Water to redirect pooled water.
	\item Levitate or Floating Disk to relieve weight.
	\item Entangle (counterintuitively useful if used to stabilize rather than restrain).
\end{itemize}
\paragraph*{Engineering Solution}
Breaking the wagon apart to reduce weight grants advantage on extraction attempts but destroys cargo value.

\subsubsection*{Possible Complications (Optional)}
The thrashing and shouting attracts attention. After 2 rounds, something stirs beneath the surface:
\begin{itemize}
	\item 2–3 Swamp Leeches (use Giant Leech stats reskinned) attempt to latch onto restrained creatures.
	\item OR
	\item A partially decomposed humanoid arm briefly rises from the mud, grasping at an ankle (purely atmospheric unless you escalate to undead)
\end{itemize}
The swamp does not attack aggressively - it reacts.

\subsubsection*{Outcomes}
\paragraph*{Success}
\begin{itemize}
	\item Horses saved.
	\item Merchants reward party with:
	\begin{itemize}
		\item 25 - 50 GP
		\item One crate of preserved rations
		\item Rumors about Paisley:\\"Old folk say the swamp used to swallow whole farms."
	\end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
\paragraph*{Partial Success}
\begin{itemize}
	\item Horses saved, wagon lost.
	\item Merchants grateful but poorer.
	\item Reward smaller (10 - 15 gp).
\end{itemize}
\paragraph*{Failure}
\begin{itemize}
	\item Horses drown.
	\item Wagon sinks entirely.
	\item Merchants devastated.
\end{itemize}
